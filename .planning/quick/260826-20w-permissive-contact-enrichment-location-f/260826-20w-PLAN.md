<objective>
Make the contact enrichment lane permissive: derived values reach HubSpot instead of being
withheld, with a review flag replacing the withholding. Add the five HubSpot-native contact
location properties to the enriched field set. Calibrate the email confidence threshold from
real execution data rather than the inherited 95.

Purpose: the operator observed a real waterfall email arrive at confidence 85 and be
discarded. HubSpot's own dedupe/merge handles identity collisions; the client does not
rebuild it. Withholding is replaced by promote-plus-flag.

Scope of "permissive" (decided here, rationale recorded): promotion into a BLANK field only.
Overwriting an existing non-blank value stays conservative — every overwrite protection
(fill_blank_only's stage_only branch, stale_refreshable's refresh-review branch,
foldContactResearch's overlap precedence, protect_if_current_present) is unchanged.

Scope of the review FLAG (decided here, rationale recorded): flag only promotions that exist
BECAUSE of this relaxation — i.e. email. phone/mobilephone/jobtitle/seniority/linkedin/persona
and the new location fields already promoted into blanks before this change and are not
flagged; flagging every blank fill would flood the triage queue with routine enrichment and
make the queue useless.

Output: two commits (location fields; permissive relaxation), a rebuilt+deployed
wf_enrichment_cloud.json, a calibration report, live disarmed proof, and a SUMMARY. No push.
