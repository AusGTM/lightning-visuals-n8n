## The three dispositions (D-5sd-04 + D-5sd-06)

Closed vocabulary, on each `attempts` entry, alongside the untouched free-prose `outcome`:

| disposition | Meaning | Eligible? |
|---|---|---|
| `empty` | the fetch succeeded and the page had no people on it | **yes** |
| `cap_exhausted` | the ladder stopped because its own fetch budget ran out | **yes** |
| `refused` | the tool returned an error code, or the page was an access-denied notice | **no** |

`refused` is ONE disposition covering every cause. `web_fetch` cannot report `robots.txt`
as a distinct cause and `extraction.md:233-236` forbids claiming it — the SKILL.md table
must never ask the model to name a cause.

**`cap_exhausted` needs an affirmative source, or D-5sd-06 never fires.** Cap exhaustion is
a property of the ladder, not of any one URL — every per-URL entry is honestly `empty`, so
without an instruction the model omits the signal and the fail-closed predicate kills
eligibility in exactly the case D-5sd-06 exists to open. SKILL.md must say: when
`next_candidates` refuses remaining candidates for budget, append **one final** `attempts`
entry carrying `disposition: "cap_exhausted"`.

## Why there is no separate "the waterfall never answered" check

D-5sd-01 condition 2 (validated through the enrichment machinery) is **already implemented**
by machinery this task does not touch. A stage-1 row is `firstname`+`lastname`+`company`+
`jobtitle` and never carries an email (`synthesise_rows`, `suggest_contacts.py:294-303`), so
a person Lusha did not resolve — no match, or `merge_report.unanswered` — still has no email
after the merge, and `partition_for_dispatch` -> `extraction.hold_emailless` holds them with
`reason_code: "no_email"`. Adding a second unanswered check would be a redundant guard over
the same rows. The ONLY new condition is D-5sd-01's condition 1, the source tier.

## One matching rule for all three tiers

`host == listed or host.endswith("." + listed)`, on the URL **host** only, after
canonicalising with `url_fallback._canonical_authority` (casefold, drop a single leading
`www.` when a dot remains). Deliberately **not** `url_fallback.same_host`: that is a fetch
guard on attacker-influenceable sitemap content and refuses subdomains, so a search hit on
the company's own `board.example.com` would fall to tier 4 and be thrown away. This is a
*source-ranking* question — whose claim to trust — and a company's own subdomain is the
company. Same direction and same suffix trap as `email_domain_relation`
(`suggest_contacts.py:484`). Importing a private helper across modules is the established
register here: `suggest_contacts.py` already imports `enrichment._clean_domain` (`:65`, `:478`).
