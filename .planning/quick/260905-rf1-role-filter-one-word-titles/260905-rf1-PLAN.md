## The decision: candidate (a), vocabulary-only, as three NEW families appended at the end

**Candidate (b) — "accept a single-token title against a family whose label starts with that
token" — is empirically dead, and this is measured, not argued.** Against the shipped
17-family vocabulary (measured 2026-09-05, this repo):

```
labels whose first token is 'marketing'    -> ['Marketing Manager']
labels whose first token is 'media'        -> []
labels whose first token is 'sponsorship'  -> []
```

(b) covers **1 of the 3 proven-live cases**. `Media` and `Sponsorship` have no family whose
label begins with that token, so (b) cannot satisfy the todo's own required test shape
(`"Media"` returns a broadcast/communications family), and it would still have to be paired
with a vocabulary edit for the other two. It also widens every existing family's reach — the
trap the todo names — to buy one third of the fix. Rejected. `role_classify.py` is not
touched by this plan.

**Candidate (a) it is — but as three new families, not as bare members bolted into the
existing graded families.** That placement choice is forced by a measured flip, not taste.
`classify_title` (`role_classify.py:152`) takes the longest matching member (strict `>` at
line 174), so an equal-length tie is broken by **family order in the YAML**. Every graded
marketing/media/broadcast family sits at index 3–7; `Board & Committee` (which owns the bare
member `Director`) sits at index 11. Measured, each variant applied on its own:

| bare member added to | `Marketing Director` / `Media Director` |
| --- | --- |
| `Head of Marketing` | `Board & Committee` → **`Head of Marketing`** (FLIP) |
| `Marketing Manager` | `Board & Committee` → **`Marketing Manager`** (FLIP) |
| `Head of Broadcast` | `Board & Committee` → **`Head of Broadcast`** (FLIP) |
| `Communications Manager` | `Board & Committee` → **`Communications Manager`** (FLIP) |
| **appended as a new family at the END** | `Board & Committee` → `Board & Committee` (no flip) |

Every in-place home breaks the "no currently-correct classification flips" constraint on the
one-token tie. Appending at the end does not, because the tie resolves to the earlier family.
**Append-at-end is therefore load-bearing, not cosmetic** — Task 1's snapshot test pins it so
a future reorder fails loudly.

The tempting alternative for `Marketing` specifically was `Head of Marketing`, and it has real
live evidence behind it: enrichment resolved that same Brisbane Roar person's richer title as
`Head of Marketing and Content`. It is still rejected — it flips `Marketing Director`, and it
would label a marketing coordinator "Head of Marketing" on every other club. An ungraded
one-word departmental title deserves an ungraded family; a graded title keeps its graded
family because longest-wins already protects it (`Head of Marketing and Content` → `Head of
Marketing`, `Marketing Manager` → `Marketing Manager`, both re-measured unchanged).

**Collision audit (the trap the todo names for (a)) — clean.** Full census of single-token
members after the edit, measured:

```
ceo·CEO  cmo·CMO  chairman·Chair  chairperson·Chair  president·President
secretary·Secretary  treasurer·Treasurer  director·Board & Committee
directors·Board & Committee  marketing·Marketing  media·Media  sponsorship·Sponsorship
```

Every token belongs to exactly one family. No new token collides with an existing one, so no
new equal-length tie exists at all. Task 1 turns this audit into a permanent test.

**Why only 3 of the todo's 7 short forms.** `communications`, `commercial`, `operations` and
`finance` are NOT added: none was observed live, `communications`/`operations` would tie-flip
if placed in their existing graded families and would otherwise add menu entries nobody has
been seen to need, and `commercial`/`finance` have no live evidence at all. They are one YAML
append away if a later sitting measures them. YAGNI, named.

**Honest limit, to be repeated in the SUMMARY, not overclaimed.** `suggest_contacts.
select_people` selects a person only when the classified label is in the operator's
round-level `chosen_families`. The UAT does not record which labels the operator ticked for
the Brisbane Roar round, so this fix does not retroactively make that round yield 3 — it makes
a selection that yields 3 **possible**, where before no selection could. The operator must tick
the new `Marketing` / `Media` / `Sponsorship` entries, which `offer_block` now lists.

**Out of scope.** The second, cheaper mitigation the todo mentions — classifying against the
richer *enriched* title rather than the scraped one — is not done here. It belongs to
`.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-04-phone-is-never-chased-only-accepted.md`, which is the todo
recording that the enriched title and seniority were both discovered and then dropped.
