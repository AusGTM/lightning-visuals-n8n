Close `.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-04-website-less-company-search-fallback.md` under its

`skills/suggest-contacts/SKILL.md`, a composition test driving the whole website-less round
end to end, and the todo moved to `completed/`.

  its suffix-trap rule unchanged (D-5sd-01, D-62-7). A website-less company simply has no
  recorded domain, so its rows are held as `company_domain_unknown` unless the operator
  supplies one (D-ad2-03). That is the honest consequence and it gets documented, not fixed.

  relatedness check as a harvested alternate. Separate ruling, explicitly out of scope.
- `hold_weak_sources`, `_host_matches`, `same_host`, `url_fallback.py` and
  `MAX_FALLBACK_SEARCHES` are unmodified.
