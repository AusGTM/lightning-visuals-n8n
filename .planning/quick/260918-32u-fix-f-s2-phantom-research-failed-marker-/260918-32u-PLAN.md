**The todo's title, premise and proposed fix are all WRONG. Do not implement them.** There is
no parser defect and no Phase 46 parity gap. `n8n/code/webResearch.js::extractFinalJson`
(lines 136-152) already strips a ```` ```json ```` fence with the regex
`/^```(?:json)?\s*|\s*```$/gm`, byte-identical to `src/web_research.py::_extract_json`.
Live re-read of executions 12526-12542 (Stage B, run `ca0537929b26401384f361902039ce7e`) on
2026-09-18: `Claude Web Research` produced 18 responses, `Validate Research Output` parsed
**18/18** — 17 `matched: true`, 1 `false` (daktronics.com, a hardware vendor, correct). NYRA
(12540), the response attempt 3 quoted as "fenced and rejected", came out
`lv_org_type: individual_club_team` at confidence 85.

The 12 `research_failed` rows are PHANTOMS — exactly one per execution that ran research
(12526, 12528, 12533-12542), each with `domain: None, hs_object_id: None`. The chain, read
from runData:

1. `IF Research Errored` carries `alwaysOutputData: true` — the ONE place in the graph where
   `set_always_output_data` (rather than a starved-lane sentinel) is correct
   (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py` ~:8676 states the rationale). It keeps
   `Build Response Merge Stage 2` input 1 fed on the "research happened, no error" case.
2. On that case the gate's TRUE branch (output 0) is empty, so n8n's
   `ensureAlwaysOutputData` pushes one literal `{ json: {} }` item down it.
3. `Build Research Failure Response` spreads that `{}` and unconditionally stamps
   `action: "research_failed"`, `gate: { reason: "research call failed" }` onto it.
4. `Filter Build Response Rows` drops a bare `{}` and drops the reserved sentinel key — but
   the item is no longer bare, and carries neither key.
5. `Build Response`'s positive `hasRowIdentity` test accepts it, because `action` IS one of
   the six identity keys.
6. `operator-claude-plugin/scripts/written_records.py` (`ACTION_TO_OUTCOME`) buckets
   `research_failed` as FAILED. Attempt 3's audit counted 12 and attributed them to a fence.

**NOT explained by this, and deliberately OUT of scope:** GRNSW (12536) researched
`lv_produces_content: False` at confidence 92 WITH evidence (it survived TS-2) and took a
`no_content` veto on a governing body; Perth Racing, Pakenham and Murray Bridge researched
`False` too. That is the research model's evidenced answer — a research-prompt quality
question about racing clubs — not a parse loss. It becomes a `question` todo in Task 2, and
no code in this plan touches the research prompt.
