## Reasoned Decisions (defaults for the planner)

1. **Fix by structural mirror, not by re-derivation.** Move the `cacheKeys[...]` write inside `if (decision === "promote")` in `mergeCompanies.js`, matching `mergeContacts.js` line-for-line in shape. Do NOT take the opportunity to refactor the two into a shared helper — they are deliberately separate files for zero-regression-risk reasons established in Phase 11, and a shared extraction would re-open that.

2. **Red-before-green is required EVIDENCE, not an intention.** The end-to-end functional validation must demonstrate the bug existing on the pre-fix build and absent on the post-fix build. Capture the failing output before the fix lands. A test written after the fix that merely passes proves the assertion compiles, not that it detects the bug.

3. **End-to-end means through the compiled node body.** Unit-level `mergeCompanies()` assertions are necessary but NOT sufficient for this phase's stated goal — the whole reason gpt #6 survived into a shipped chain is that the offline suite exercises JS functions in isolation. Drive a stale-unpromoted companies row through the emitted `Merge Company` jsCode using the `researchChainRowFlow.test.mjs` mechanism (extract jsCode from the built workflow, execute it, inspect the emitted `cacheKeys`).

4. **The frozen-fixture re-baseline is its own reviewed commit.** Separate commit from the source fix, with a message that states WHY the fixture changed and WHAT the diff is bounded to. Before re-baselining, prove the diff touches ONLY the `Merge Company` node — the other six frozen nodes must be byte-identical pre/post. Do not re-baseline by blind overwrite; diff first, assert the shape of the change, then write.

5. **Both builders are in scope.** The fixture pins `cloud` AND `local_live`. `Merge Company` appears in both; the fix propagates to both via the shared inlined module.

6. **Rebuild is mandatory and deterministic.** Editing `n8n/code/*.js` regenerates `wf_enrichment_cloud.json` / `wf_enrichment_local_live.json`. Rebuild, commit the regenerated JSON, and confirm determinism (rebuild twice → no diff).
