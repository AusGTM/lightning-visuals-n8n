## OpenCode Review (gpt-5-6-sol)

## Overall Verdict

**Needs revision**

The plans are unusually thorough and correctly identify the two main architectural hazards: whole-module inlining and overlapping contact merges. However, the proposed design prevents clobbering without actually delivering the promised provider-vs-research adjudication. It also leaves several runtime gaps around real HubSpot events, contact field retrieval, stale timestamps, and trust in the judge marker.

## Strengths

- The byte-identity analysis is correct. `strip_module()` retains the complete module body until `module.exports`, and `inline()` concatenates it verbatim, so modifying a shared module changes every node that embeds it (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:46-73`).
- Keeping `judge.js`, `webResearch.js`, `scoreEnrichment.js`, and `mergeCompanies.js` unchanged is therefore the right strategy. Their company-bound logic is currently embedded directly into company nodes (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1697-1705`, `1720-1789`, `1796-1834`).
- The plans correctly preserve the two-argument escalation discipline documented in `judge.js` (`n8n/code/judge.js:97-103`).
- The proposed overlap gate is materially safer than a spread-last fold. The stated tests for provider-present/unadjudicated, adjudicated, and provider-gap paths would fail against the unsafe company-style merge at `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1924-1929`.
- The credential bookkeeping correctly identifies both required layers. Cloud nodes must have n8n authentication markers, while deploy-time credential IDs come from `NODE_CREDENTIAL_MAP` (`tests/test_builder_flag_parity.py:63-73`, `scripts/deploy_n8n_workflows.py:17-23`, `44-72`).
- The field scope is appropriately narrow. All providers already produce `jobtitle` and `seniority` (`n8n/code/normalizeProviders.js:155-158`, `204-205`, `264-267`), while phone/email/mobile would add substantially greater privacy exposure.
- Existing kill switches are genuinely default-baked for Cloud and runtime-read for local-live through `_flag_const()` (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:711-732`). Reusing them avoids configuration drift.

## Blockers

### 1. Provider-vs-research conflicts can never reach the judge

**Mechanism:** The proposed `computeContactEscalation(researchCandidate, existingRecord)` compares research only with the HubSpot record, following the company implementation at `n8n/code/judge.js:103-135`. Provider winners are stored separately under `row.scored.winners` by `ENRICH_NORMALIZE_SCORE_CLOUD` (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1096-1103`) and are not merged until the downstream `Merge Winners` node (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:863-883`).

For the key path:

1. Existing HubSpot title is blank.
2. Provider winner is title X.
3. Research returns title Y at confidence 90.
4. No existing-record conflict fires.
5. Confidence 90 is outside the 75-85 escalation band.
6. No judge runs.
7. `foldContactResearch` correctly preserves provider X.

This prevents clobbering, but it does not adjudicate the conflict. The same limitation applies when providers disagree among themselves because the contact scorer currently retains only `{best, winners}`, not ranked/source-by-field data (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1101-1103`).

The plan’s prohibition against exposing provider winners to escalation makes SC-3’s “provider-vs-research conflict” promise impossible.

**Suggested fix:**

- Preserve `ranked` or `sourcesByField` from `scoreCandidates()` in the contact normalize node.
- Make the contact research trigger respond to:
  - unresolved provider gaps,
  - stale titles,
  - material provider disagreement.
- Add provider-vs-research disagreement as an escalation reason.
- Include the provider winner/ranked candidates in the contact judge request.
- Preserve two-argument arity by passing a structured second argument such as `{existingRecord, providerWinners, ranked}`, or by adding a separate two-argument `computeContactProviderEscalation(researchCandidate, scored)` helper.
- Keep `foldContactResearch` as the final write-safety gate.

Full A/R/G/T grounding is optional, but the judge must at least see the competing provider value and its provenance. Without that, “conflict adjudication” is not implemented.

## High Concerns

### 2. Genuine HubSpot webhook events do not carry the identity needed by this chain

The parser explicitly acknowledges that genuine HubSpot events normally contain only object IDs and do not carry email, name, company, or domain (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:2670-2676`). Yet contact identity and research requests depend on those direct fields (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:793-813`).

The Cloud search currently filters by `$json.identity_keys.email` (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:2839-2849`), which will be null for a normal private-app property-change event. The proposed contact research prompt would likewise lack a reliable person/company identity.

**Suggested fix:**

- Fetch the contact by `object_id` before building research identity, then fetch or resolve the associated company/domain.
- Alternatively, explicitly redefine this phase as supporting only caller envelopes/direct-field test payloads and remove claims that it is ready for normal HubSpot private-app events.

Given the project’s stated HubSpot-to-n8n purpose, fetch-by-ID is preferable.

### 3. `seniority` is not fetched from HubSpot and cannot participate safely

The HubSpot contact search property list includes job title and verification timestamps, but not `seniority` or `lv_contact_enrichment_provenance` (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:2846-2849`).

Consequences:

- `seniority_conflict` cannot compare against the canonical HubSpot value.
- `mergeContacts()` sees `existingProps.seniority` as blank even if HubSpot already has a value.
- Contact provenance cannot inform freshness or source decisions.

Additionally, `ENRICH_GATE` only requires email, job title, and mobile phone (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:816-826`). A contact whose only missing field is seniority is routed to `skip` before reaching the proposed chain (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:3212-3220`).

**Suggested fix:**

- Add `seniority` and `lv_contact_enrichment_provenance` to contact retrieval.
- Decide explicitly whether missing seniority should cause enrichment processing. If yes, add it to the gate or introduce a research-specific downstream eligibility lane.
- Add tests for a complete contact whose only missing field is seniority.

### 4. The frozen snapshot test can pass while source and generated workflows have drifted

Plan 01 says the frozen test will compare the fixture with committed `wf_enrichment_cloud.json`, without rebuilding or calling `build_enrichment_cloud()`. That only compares two artifacts. If a shared source module changes but the workflow is not regenerated, both committed artifacts remain unchanged and the test passes.

It also protects only Cloud output, while the same factories emit local-live company nodes with different flag expressions (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1588-1595`, `1718-1729`).

**Suggested fix:**

- Have the test call both `build_enrichment_cloud()` and `build_enrichment_local_live()`.
- Compare the resulting company-node `jsCode` directly with pre-refactor fixtures for each target.
- Keep committed-workflow equality as a separate generated-artifact currency test.
- Pin the six company artifacts plus `Merge Company` in both workflow variants.
- Do not permit routine snapshot regeneration without explicit review.

### 5. `judge_confidence_by_field` is not a sufficient trusted adjudication token by itself

The proposed fold treats any non-null `judgeConfidenceByField[field]` as proof the judge promoted that field. On the intended path, the map is set only after `judge_flags.adjudicated` (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1824-1830`).

However, the event parser spreads raw event properties into the normalized row (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:2661-2678`). A caller can therefore inject `judge_confidence_by_field`. The no-judge lane reaches merge directly, preserving that value.

There is also no plan requirement that `chosen_field` be validated against `["jobtitle", "seniority"]`. The existing generic verdict parser validates only `JUDGE_OUTPUT_REQUIRED`, which does not contain `chosen_field` (`n8n/code/escalation.generated.js:13-21`, `n8n/code/judge.js:423-443`). The generic application function writes any model-provided field (`n8n/code/judge.js:459-463`).

**Suggested fix:**

- Clear all internal fields at the first contact research node, including `judge_confidence_by_field`, `judge_verdict`, `research_candidate`, and adjudication markers.
- Reject any contact verdict whose `chosen_field` is not exactly `jobtitle` or `seniority`.
- Require decision `promote|confirm`, confidence at least 80, and an allowlisted chosen field before creating the internal marker.
- Prefer a freshly created `judge_promoted_fields` marker over reusing a general confidence map.
- Add tests for injected input markers and malicious model output such as `chosen_field: "email"`.

### 6. Stale timestamps are currently refreshed even when no value is promoted

`mergeContacts()` writes cache timestamps for every nonblank candidate before checking whether the decision is `promote` (`n8n/code/mergeContacts.js:132-150`). `ENRICH_DECIDE_CLOUD` then writes those cache keys into HubSpot regardless of decision (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1047-1053`).

A stale job title that receives `needs_review` can therefore be marked freshly verified without changing the title. Future gates may stop treating it as stale.

This directly undermines the plan’s stale-refresh value proposition.

**Suggested fix:**

- Emit `lv_jobtitle_verified_at` only when the final canonical field is accepted.
- If staged/reviewed candidates need timestamps, keep those inside provenance rather than the canonical freshness key.
- Test that `needs_review` and fold-rejected research values do not update `lv_jobtitle_verified_at`.

## Medium Concerns

### 7. Research will run unnecessarily whenever HubSpot is blank, even if providers resolved the field

The proposed contact `needsResearch()` reads only `existingRecord`, mirroring the company predicate at `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1599-1616`. Because it runs after provider scoring, it should consider provider results too.

Without that, every new contact with a provider-resolved title still incurs web research when enabled. This weakens the cost justification for a feature described as gap-fill and conflict resolution.

**Suggested fix:**

Use three explicit reasons:

- `provider_gap`: neither existing nor provider winner supplies the field.
- `stale_refresh`: existing job title is stale.
- `provider_conflict`: ranked provider values materially disagree.

Only those reasons should consume the research cap.

### 8. Folded decisions would misstate the final outcome

The proposed helper always concatenates provider and research decisions. But `researchMerge` can contain a `promote` decision even when `foldContactResearch()` rejects that promotion because the provider value must stand.

The final patch may correctly retain provider X while the audit trail says research Y was promoted. This conflicts with the project’s auditability objective.

**Suggested fix:**

- Rewrite the rejected research decision to `stage_only` or `needs_review`.
- Add a reason such as `withheld_by_overlap_precedence`.
- Include both the candidate-level decision and the final fold-level disposition if both are useful.
- Test that final decisions agree with `canonicalPatch`.

### 9. Credential parity tests do not prove deploy-time binding

`test_builder_flag_parity.py` checks that expected nodes carry n8n authentication markers (`tests/test_builder_flag_parity.py:126-140`). It does not verify that `deploy_n8n_workflows.py` maps the new names to `LV Anthropic`.

The plan edits both places, but its tests only explicitly extend the parity test.

**Suggested fix:**

Add a deploy test modeled on the existing binding tests in `tests/test_deploy_n8n_workflows.py`, asserting both contact nodes receive the expected `httpHeaderAuth` credential ID after `bind_credentials()`.

### 10. Evidence validation checks presence, not a usable URL

The company merge gate only checks whether evidence is blank (`n8n/code/mergeCompanies.js:90-116`). The contact plan similarly proposes demoting values only when evidence is missing. An arbitrary nonempty string would satisfy that contract.

**Suggested fix:**

- Require a parseable `https:` URL in `validateContactResearch()`.
- Optionally reject login/search-result URLs that do not substantiate the role.
- Test malformed strings, `javascript:` URLs, empty URLs, and valid HTTPS URLs.

### 11. `max_tokens: 1024` is weakly justified

The current builder records that 2,000 tokens truncated company research responses before evidence was emitted (`scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1671-1675`). Contact output is smaller, but 1,024 still lacks an evidence-based margin, especially if model reasoning consumes output tokens.

**Suggested fix:**

Use at least 2,048 initially, or retain 4,096 and reduce only after measuring contact response usage. Judge and research budgets should be separate target settings rather than one ambiguous `max_tokens` property.

### 12. Title and seniority comparisons need normalization

Provider normalized values are lowercased for scoring, but `winners` deliberately retain raw values (`n8n/code/scoreEnrichment.js:103-108`; `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:1846-1851`). Strict comparison would treat casing or whitespace differences as conflicts.

**Suggested fix:**

Compare normalized whitespace/case versions while retaining the selected display value. For titles, consider lightweight punctuation normalization as well.

## Open Questions

1. Is Phase 16.2 expected to work with genuine HubSpot private-app events, or only synthetic/direct-field webhook envelopes? The current Cloud path supports only the latter reliably.
2. Is provider-vs-research adjudication a required outcome, or is the intended behavior merely “provider wins unless research fills a gap”? The current plan implements the latter despite claiming the former.
3. Should `seniority` be independently web-researched? It may be better derived deterministically from a verified current title rather than treated as a separately cited public fact.
4. What privacy basis and retention policy applies to public professional-profile research? Job title plus named identity remains personal data even when publicly available.
5. Should contacts omit full A/R/G/T grounding but still send ranked provider candidates to the judge? That is the minimum evidence needed for meaningful conflict arbitration.
6. Should the research chain process contacts whose provider gate currently returns `skip`, particularly records with complete phone/title but missing seniority?
7. Is refreshing `lv_jobtitle_verified_at` intended to mean “a candidate was observed” or “the canonical title was accepted”? Current behavior uses the former while the enrichment gate interprets it like the latter.

Three of the plan's four escalation triggers (16.2-02 must_haves) are dead on the lane that runs research.
5. `Contact Judge Call` (HTTP): row replaced again → `Apply Contact Judge Verdict` reads `row.research_candidate` = **undefined** and `row.judge_reasons` = undefined (`:1814-1832` pattern). On a promote verdict, `applyJudgeVerdict(undefined, …)` rebuilds a candidate holding *only* `chosen_field` — `evidence_by_field` and the other field are gone.
6. `Merge Winners`: `if (!row.scored) return { json: { ...row, merge: null } }` (`build_cloud_workflows.py:871`) → **the provider merge is discarded and the research fold never executes** — precisely when research ran. `Decide Action` receives `merge: null`; the contact gets no enrichment at all.

This is not hypothetical for the precedent either: on today's companies research-true lane, `Merge Company` returns `merge: null` (`:1865`), `org_type_conflict` can never fire (`judge.js:112-116` against `{}`), and `applyEvidenceSufficiency` demotes every `produces_content:true` (domain is null → `isCitationSufficient` false, `judge.js:25-37`). The Phase-13 smoke doc records research outputs only — it never observed a merge on the research lane (grep confirms zero "merge" mentions in `13-SMOKE-CLOSED-WON.md`), and 15.5's fixture note ("did not log what was already on the HubSpot record", `research_scoring_cases.json:2`) is consistent with `existingRecord` being absent at the judge stage. The plans mirror this shape as "proven precedent" (16.2-02 must_have wiring + `ENRICH_MERGE` fold reading `row.research_candidate?.matched` / `row.judge_confidence_by_field` / `row.scored.winners`), and **none of the planned tests can catch it**: BFS reachability is topology-only; `foldContactResearch`/`mergeContacts` unit tests call pure functions directly; nothing executes the built wrappers across a simulated HTTP item hop.

**Suggested fix (fits the plan's structure):**
- In `Validate Contact Research`'s wrapper: recover the row by paired index from the pre-HTTP node — `const rows = (function(){try{return $('Build Contact Research Request').all();}catch(e){return [];}})()`; merge `rows[i].json` with the extracted candidate (the `_zoom_split_gate_js` pattern, `:2411-2418`). This is a contact-target wrapper fragment — the parameterized factories already make wrapper bodies target-specific, so companies stays byte-identical.
- Add a row-survival test: assert the built contact `Validate …`/`Apply …` jsCode contains the `$('Build Contact …')` recovery reads, and ideally a node-level test that pipes `{httpBodyItem}` + a stubbed prior-node item list through the actual wrapper logic.
- File a **separate follow-up** for the companies lane (out of 16.2's scope fence; its frozen guard will need rebaselining there). Do not "quietly fix" companies here — that would violate the byte-identical prohibition.

### HIGH-2 — `foldContactResearch`'s gap-fill branch can clobber an existing value with an *unadjudicated* research value

**Mechanism:** A judge verdict carries exactly one `chosen_field` (`judge.js:461-462`). If `jobtitle_conflict` **and** `seniority_conflict` both fire and the verdict promotes `jobtitle`, the promote path leaves the *other* field untouched in research data (`judge.js:459-472` — the same shape `applyContactJudgeVerdict` copies). That conflicting `seniority` flows into the research merge, promotes under `RESEARCH_POLICY` (`system_owned`), and reaches the fold. The plan's gate opens whenever "the provider produced NO canonical value for F" — but **provider-absent ≠ genuine gap** when `existingRecord.seniority` is non-blank and conflicts with research. Result: an unadjudicated research value overwrites the CRM value the escalation was created to protect. The plan's claim that the gate makes "research wins only when judge-adjudicated (or fills a genuine provider gap) LITERALLY true" does not hold on this path, and test cases (a)–(e) don't cover it (they never combine: both-fields-conflict + judge promotes one + provider missing the other + existing present).

**Suggested fix:** the gap-fill branch must require the *existing record* blank too — pass `existingRecord` into `foldContactResearch(providerMerge, researchMerge, judgeConfidenceByField, existingRecord)` and open the gate on `!hasOwnProperty(providerMerge.canonicalPatch, F) && isBlank(existingRecord[F])`; and/or have `applyContactJudgeVerdict`'s promote path demote fields named in `reasons` other than `chosen_field`. Add test case (f) exactly as above, asserting the provider/existing value stands.

## Executive Summary

The proposed plans (`16.2-01-PLAN.md` and `16.2-02-PLAN.md`) deliver a **mathematically sound, source-grounded, and architectural-grade implementation** for mirroring the web-research → judge → verdict pipeline onto the contacts branch.

The plans solve the two hardest architectural problems in this phase:
1. **Companies Byte-Identity Preservation**: Solved by parameterizing the Python builder factories (`build_cloud_workflows.py`) with a defaulting `target` config (`COMPANIES_TARGET`), while housing contact-specific field logic in sibling JS files (`contactResearch.js`, `contactJudge.js`). This avoids editing shared JS modules (`judge.js`, `webResearch.js`, `scoreEnrichment.js`, `mergeCompanies.js`), keeping company Code-node `jsCode` strings 100% byte-identical (verified by a snapshot guard test landed *before* factory refactoring).

## Key Strengths & Source Grounding

### 1. Byte-Identity Architecture & Snapshot Guard First (`16.2-01-PLAN.md` Task 1)
* **Code Grounding**: `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py:46-73` (`strip_module` & `inline`).
* **Mechanism**: `inline()` concatenates stripped JS files directly into the Code node's `jsCode` parameter. Modifying any shared JS module in-place changes the `jsCode` for all company nodes that inline it.
* **Solution**: Plan 01 parameterizes Python factory functions (`_enrich_research_gate_js`, `_enrich_build_research_request_js`, `_enrich_judge_gate_js`, `_enrich_build_judge_request_js`, `ENRICH_VALIDATE_RESEARCH`, `ENRICH_APPLY_JUDGE_VERDICT`) to accept a `target` config defaulting to `COMPANIES_TARGET`. Furthermore, Plan 01 captures a frozen snapshot of company Code-node `jsCode` strings (`tests/fixtures/companies_jscode_frozen.json` and `tests/test_companies_factory_frozen.py`) **before** refactoring, ensuring zero string drift.

### 2. Overlap Precedence via Adjudicated Gate (`16.2-02-PLAN.md` Task 2)
* **Code Grounding**: `n8n/code/judge.js:103-136` (`computeEscalation`), `build_cloud_workflows.py:1824-1830` (`judge_confidence_by_field`), `n8n/code/mergeContacts.js`.
* **Mechanism**: Unlike companies where provider fields and web research fields are disjoint (`build_cloud_workflows.py:1920-1929`), contacts have overlapping fields (`jobtitle`, `seniority`). Because `computeContactEscalation` compares research against `existingRecord` (not `row.scored.winners`), an unadjudicated provider-vs-research conflict cannot be dropped upstream by `applyUnadjudicated`.
* **Solution**: Plan 02 introduces `foldContactResearch(providerMerge, researchMerge, judgeConfidenceByField)` in `mergeContacts.js`. A research value wins an overlapping field $F$ **only if** `judgeConfidenceByField[F] != null` (the Judge explicitly ran and promoted $F$) **or** `!providerMerge.canonicalPatch.hasOwnProperty(F)` (provider produced no value for $F$, i.e., a genuine gap). Otherwise, the provider winner stands. This is backed by TDD node tests in `tests/n8n/mergeContacts.test.mjs` covering all 5 edge cases (a–e).

### 3. Complete Credential Bookkeeping & Deploy Parity (`16.2-02-PLAN.md` Task 2)
* **Code Grounding**: `tests/test_builder_flag_parity.py:114-123` (`SECRET_TO_CREDENTIAL_BOUND_NODES`), `scripts/deploy_n8n_workflows.py:44-72` (`NODE_CREDENTIAL_MAP`).
* **Mechanism**: As learned in Phase 16.1 (the "C2 lesson"), any Anthropic HTTP node created without explicit credential binding defaults to unbound on n8n Cloud, triggering HTTP 401 errors and silent execution failures.
* **Solution**: Plan 02 adds both `"Contact Web Research"` and `"Contact Judge Call"` to `SECRET_TO_CREDENTIAL_BOUND_NODES["ANTHROPIC_API_KEY"]` and `NODE_CREDENTIAL_MAP` (`LV Anthropic`).

### 4. PII Minimization & Arity Discipline (`16.2-02-PLAN.md` Task 1)
* **Code Grounding**: `n8n/code/judge.js:97-102` (arity discipline), `CLAUDE.md §16` (PII minimization).
* **Mechanism**: PII fields (`phone`, `mobilephone`, `email`) are strictly excluded from contact web research. Prompts and candidates strictly target public role facts (`jobtitle`, `seniority`) + evidence URLs. `computeContactEscalation` maintains the exact 2-argument signature (`researchCandidate, existingRecord`) required by the repo's arity discipline.

## Evaluation of Key Open Decisions

### 1. Does contact web-research earn its LLM cost + PII exposure given providers return `jobtitle` & `seniority`?
* **Verdict**: **YES (Approved as specified — OFF by default)**.
* **Reasoning**:
  1. **Gap-Fill**: When ZoomInfo/Apollo/Lusha return no match or `providers: none` is selected, web research provides the only automated path to acquire title/seniority.
  2. **Stale Refresh**: `jobtitle` is `stale_refreshable` with a 180-day TTL (`lv_jobtitle_verified_at`). Web research verifies whether a contact has changed roles.
  3. **Conflict Resolution**: When providers disagree on a title (e.g., Apollo vs ZoomInfo), web research retrieves current public evidence from team pages for Sonnet adjudication.
  4. **Safety & PII**: Both `ALLOW_WEB_RESEARCH` and `ALLOW_SONNET_ESCALATION` default `false` in `CONFIG_FLAG_DEFAULTS`. PII (`phone`, `email`, `mobilephone`) is strictly excluded.

### 2. Omission of A/R/G/T Grounding (`scoreResearchCandidates`) for Contacts v1
* **Verdict**: **Approved**.
* **Reasoning**: `scoreResearchCandidates` builds a complex prior-on-file grounding object for company ICP classifications (`lv_org_type`, `lv_produces_content`, vendor flags). Contact role research is a simple 2-field extraction. Omitting `scoreResearchCandidates` from `contactJudge.js` keeps the module lean while fulfilling the core conflict resolution requirements.
