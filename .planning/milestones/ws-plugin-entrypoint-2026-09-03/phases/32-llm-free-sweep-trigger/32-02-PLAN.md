session open, and be loud when it cannot run.

until a cron daemon fires it with nobody watching.

requirement/state rows set to the observed truth — pass or fail.
</objective>

32-01 shipped `skills/backend-sweep/lv-sweep-run.sh`: a POSIX `sh` wrapper that runs
`scripts/sweep_entry.py` directly under a named Python, appends one stamped line to the log when
the backend is healthy, appends the full notice JSON and posts one `osascript` banner per
headline when it is not, and exits non-zero AND posts a banner when it cannot run at all. No
LLM, no Anthropic credential, nothing outside `/usr/bin:/bin` plus the named interpreter.
`SWEEP-CRON-TEMPLATE.md` was rewritten around it and the wrapper's contract with
`sweep_entry.py`'s printed output is pinned by `tests/test_sweep_trigger_contract.py`.

Everything above is proven at the source level. **Nothing above is proven under cron.** That is
what this checkpoint is for, and it is the precise mistake this phase exists to correct.
  </what-built>

1. Install from the shipped `SWEEP-CRON-TEMPLATE.md` verbatim — venv, then the crontab line
   calling `lv-sweep-run.sh` with its three arguments. Note whether the crontab was empty first.
2. Let a real cron fire land **with no session open**. Check
   `~/Library/Logs/lv-backend-sweep.log`.
   - Healthy → exactly one stamped line, no banner.
   - Notices → the count, the full JSON, one banner per headline.

   session open".
5. Step 5: restore the cadence, the interpreter argument, and the crontab to its prior state.
6. Step 6: confirm from n8n's execution history and the provider balances that the firings wrote
   nothing and consumed no credits.

Report back: the log lines observed (verbatim), the fire timestamps, whether a banner appeared
