<objective>
Give review writeback its own backend arming flag, `ALLOW_HUBSPOT_REVIEW_WRITES`, separate from
dispatch arming in both directions (D-01, D-02, D-08e). Without this, a client-side "review is
armed" boolean would be a fiction: the only real gate on a HubSpot write in this repo is the baked
write-safety constant a write gate reads, and today that constant is the dispatch one.

Purpose: make REVIEW-03's "its own session-scoped confirmation, separate from dispatch arming" true
at the backend, not just in the conversation. Phase 28's arm/dispatch/disarm cycle flips
`ALLOW_HUBSPOT_RECORD_WRITES`; nothing it arms may enable a review write, and nothing armed here may
enable a dispatch write.

Output: a fifth write-safety constant, a `review` action branch in the shared `_writeSafetyAllows`
gate function, the deploy-time overlay entry that arms it, the deliberately-updated pinned assertion
that previously fixed the overlayable set at four names, and an executable separation test.
</objective>
