# Phase 62 Plan 02: The portal's own job titles, clustered once into a committed cache Summary

**`scripts/role_vocabulary.py` (repo root, credential- and portal-guarded) sweeps live contact `jobtitle` values and clusters them with one cached Haiku call into a committed YAML; `role_classify.py` gained the loader/offer/select trio that carries the vocabulary's evidence status all the way to the operator-facing menu, and SUGGEST-03 was amended — not closed — to permit that disclosed fallback.**

## Accomplishments
- `scripts/role_vocabulary.py`: a read-only, paged sweep of every contact's `jobtitle` (`POST /crm/v3/objects/contacts/search`), credential-guarded (`_has_credentials()` skip-to-exit-0) and portal-guarded (`_portal_ok()` refuse-with-no-API-call), mirroring `inventory_org_type_values.py`'s exact idiom. Below `SPARSE_THRESHOLD` distinct titles it serves the disclosed generic fallback and never calls Haiku; otherwise it makes ONE clustering call, ranks families by summed recurrence, and keeps the top `TOP_N_FAMILIES` (8).
- `operator-claude-plugin/config/role_vocabulary.yaml`: seeded by actually invoking `build_generic_fallback()` and writing its output — the committed cache is the real generic-fallback branch's output, not a hand-authored approximation of it. `evidenced: false` at document AND every family level.
- `role_classify.load_families()`/`offer_block()`/`chosen_families()`: the loader that never separates the family list from its evidence status, the renderer whose disclosure sentence is D-62-07's whole mitigation, and the round-level (never per-record) selection validator. `classify_title()` itself untouched — still pure, still takes the family list as a parameter, proven by a test that points `DEFAULT_VOCABULARY_PATH` at a nonexistent file and shows classification still works.
- SUGGEST-03 amended in `v1.1-REQUIREMENTS.md` with an inline note naming D-62-07, the date, and the operator as authority; SUGGEST-01/-02/-04/-05 left byte-unchanged. `ROADMAP.md`'s Phase 62 entry now distinguishes the four closed requirements from the one amended requirement — the blanket `Closes SUGGEST-01..05` claim is gone.

## Decisions Made
- **`role_vocabulary.py` lives at the repo root, not under `operator-claude-plugin/scripts/`** — re-confirmed by grep both before writing the file and after, per PATTERNS.md's deliberate deviation from where it might otherwise be expected.
- **The seed YAML was generated by executing `build_generic_fallback()`, not hand-typed** — guarantees the committed cache is byte-identical to what the live script would produce for the identical branch, closing any drift risk between "what Task 1 says the fallback looks like" and "what actually ships."
- **`cluster_titles()`'s payload is `{"titles": [...]}` only** — no company names, no record ids, no email addresses ever enter the clustering request, satisfying T-62-07 by construction rather than by discipline.
- **`rank_top_families()` filters returned members against the sampled title set** — a defensive backstop for T-62-09 even though the system prompt already forbids invented members; an invented member is silently dropped rather than trusted.
