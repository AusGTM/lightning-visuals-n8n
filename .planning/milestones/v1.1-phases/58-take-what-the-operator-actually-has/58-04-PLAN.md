<objective>
Make backend domain research a thing the operator can see the price of and say no to, and —
if the operator chose that at 58-02's checkpoint — teach the backend research node to seek a
company's own website.

Purpose: INPUT-02 — when the input carries no usable domain, the system finds one rather than
asking the operator to. The roadmap's own risk note for this phase is that research costs money
per company, so it must be priced in the envelope and be declinable, or a bulk list quietly
becomes a research bill.

Output: a rate entry, a declinable envelope line, the per-row research decision, and either the
backend prompt/schema extension with its live proof or a written deferral of INPUT-02's
residual.
</objective>
