<objective>
Close gaps 2 and 4 of `59-VERIFICATION.md`. Gap 2: `written_records.json` is one shared path
with no concurrency protection, and `append_chunk`'s replace-not-merge rule silently drops the
loser of a race between two real shipped processes. Gap 4: a single-lane grant never tells the
operator the artifact exists.

Purpose: D-59-09 rules one artifact per `run_id` — the hazard is removed by construction rather
than by discipline, with no contention and no stale-lock failure mode on a path that must never
block a dispatch. D-59-07 requires the artifact be disclosed at every grant, because it is
written at every grant.

Both gaps land together because they meet in one sentence: the `write_grant._consequence` line
that names the artifact is both the sentence gap 4 must move and a reader gap 2 must reword.

Output: per-run artifacts, a globbing reader, every reader updated, and a disclosure that fires
for one lane as well as two.
</objective>
