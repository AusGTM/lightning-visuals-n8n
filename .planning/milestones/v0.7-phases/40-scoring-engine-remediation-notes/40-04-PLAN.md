<objective>
Add the two missing scoring components. D-06 keeps the existing component architecture — per-input
`*_score` mapper flows feeding a calculated sum — and fixes F1 by adding rather than consolidating.
This plan adds `produces_content_score` (ENGINE-02, the +20 that was absent from every component,
workflow and formula) and `gambling_score` (ENGINE-05, moving the -20 off org-type points, where
40-01 already zeroed it, onto its own `lv_is_gambling_operator`-driven component).

D-06 says "5 components total". Three exist today (`org_type_score`, `geography_score`,
`annual_revenue_score`); this plan adds the fourth and fifth. The fifth is the gambling deduction:
ENGINE-05 requires it be driven by `lv_is_gambling_operator` independent of org type, and within
D-06's component architecture there is nowhere else for it to live. That reconciliation is recorded
here rather than assumed silently.

The riskiest step is Task 3's `calculationFormula` PATCH — 40-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3 documents a
community-reported history of 400s on formulas referencing other properties. 40-01 already fetched
this portal's exact working formula string into PORTAL-FACTS.md precisely so this task extends known
syntax instead of reconstructing it from documentation.

Purpose: Tier A is unreachable until the content term exists — 40 + 10 + 10 caps at 60, which is why
a textbook Tier-A record grades B today.
Output: two properties, two mapper flows, one extended formula, all live-validated on disposables.
</objective>
