<objective>
Derive the genuinely-orphaned scoring artifacts from a live portal diff, archive the uncontested
ones reversibly, surface the ambiguous ones to the operator, and prove afterwards that the live
scoring engine is untouched.

This is the only plan in Phase 42 that mutates the portal. Plans 01 and 02 were read-only by
construction; this one is separately labelled, separately gated, and runs last so that plan 02's
expanded manifest can serve as an additional protection oracle — a property declared in
`config/hubspot_properties.yaml` is by definition not an orphan.

**F1 — the do-not-archive set, restated, because this is the plan that can violate it.** These
are NEVER archived, disabled, or otherwise touched:

- Company properties: `org_type_score`, `geography_score`, `annual_revenue_score`,
  `produces_content_score`, `gambling_score`, `lv_icp_fit_score`, `lv_icp_tier`,
  `lv_anti_icp_flag`, `lv_org_type`, `lv_produces_content`, `lv_country_region_normalized`
- Automation flows: `4626124224`, `4626722240`, `4626722237`, `4625147345`, `4634822079`,
  `4634822085`

Under the fix-in-place path these ARE the live scoring engine. ROADMAP SC1's archive list was
written when a rebuild was the presumed path and is reinterpreted by `42-CONTEXT.md` D-01, which
is authoritative. **`39-DECISION.md:107-112` is stale, superseded prose** that read literally
points at archiving the engine — do not follow it. There is no separate placeholder property to
retire; the fit-score formula was edited in place on the same record.

**F4 — the detector must fail safe.** The repo's only pre-existing reference detector matches a
namespace prefix that structurally cannot match the five component score names. A detector built
on that pattern would report the live engine as referenced by nothing and hand it to the
archival path. This plan's detector uses an explicit protection list plus a live
`calculationFormula` substring check, and classifies anything it cannot positively resolve as
ambiguous rather than as an orphan.

**Zero orphans is success.** Research's evidence-backed expectation is that the live diff finds
few or no genuine orphans — every Phase 40 disposable was torn down in-run, the Phase 21 probe
property's own cleanup step archived it, and Phase 40 edited flows in place rather than creating
copies. An empty candidate list fully satisfies CLEAN-01 and must be recorded as a result, not
treated as a failure to find something.

**n8n independence.** Do NOT run `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py` or
`scripts/deploy_n8n_workflows.py`. Disabling an orphaned HubSpot automation flow via
`/automation/v4/flows` is a portal-side operation and touches no n8n content. A content rebake
would reset the deployed write-safety constants to disarmed and could silently close Phase 41's
live import arm window.

Purpose: close CLEAN-01's archival half with evidence, without letting a false-positive orphan
disable the engine Phase 40 just repaired.
Output: a read-only-by-default derivation tool, its offline safety tests, a committed candidate
report, any archived definitions under a dated directory, and a post-mutation snapshot and drift
report proving the engine survived.
</objective>
