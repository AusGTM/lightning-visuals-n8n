<objective>
Act on 63-03's verdict: either ship the cheaper-model routing for the `confidence_band`-only class
(D-63-05) or record the lever as evaluated and dropped (D-63-06). Both are executable branches of
this plan; neither is an afterthought.

Purpose: the judge costs 16.1s of a 34.2s wall clock and `confidence_band` fires on essentially
every record carrying a classification signal, so the gate does not discriminate among the records
that reach it. Lever 2 changes who adjudicates that undifferentiated class without changing what
gets adjudicated — which is why it is the lever this phase takes and lever 1 is not.

Output: on SHIP, a second model constant, a target-scoped routing branch, regenerated workflows and
a routing test. On DROP, a drop record and an amended todo, with the builder untouched.

**What "dropped" concretely means.** Nothing about this lever is committed before the verdict is
read. So a DROP is not a revert: `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py` is simply never edited, no
workflow is regenerated for this reason, and no `git revert` is needed. What gets produced instead
is a dated record naming the verdict, the corpus it rested on, and which of the two DROP reasons
fired. The phase then seals with 63-A landed and 63-B's measurement banked — D-63-06 permits this
outcome explicitly, and 63-05's deploy still has its own reason to exist.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization` — a second judge model is introduced where there was exactly one.

**Noun now primary:** the judge model remains a per-invocation SELECTION, not a per-record
attribute. The generalized identity is "the model chosen for this judge call", and it is derived
from `judge_reasons`, which the row already carries.

**Decision: `add-alongside`, deliberately.** `ANTHROPIC_JUDGE_MODEL` stays the primary and the
default; `ANTHROPIC_JUDGE_MODEL_CHEAP` is added beside it as the exception for one evidenced class.
Promoting a general "model policy" abstraction would be premature: there is exactly one exception,
its evidence covers exactly one record class on exactly one lane, and the whole change is a build-
time string plus a ternary.

**Accepted debt, and what would force a promote:** a third model, or a second lane earning its own
routing, or any per-record (as opposed to per-reason-set) model choice. At that point the ternary
becomes a mapping from reason-set to model and belongs in one named function rather than inline in
the emitted JS.
</assumption_delta_decision>
