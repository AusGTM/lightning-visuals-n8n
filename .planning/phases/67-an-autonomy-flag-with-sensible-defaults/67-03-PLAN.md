<objective>
Give the two batch skills that have no end-of-run account one, and correct the two paragraphs
that say one of them should not have it.

Purpose: AUTO-06 makes the end-of-run report mandatory under autonomy, because with nobody
watching it is the only account of what happened. `enrich-before-ingest` and `enrich-records`
already call it; `contact-upload` and `suggest-contacts` do not (D-67-11). Both of the existing
call sites additionally carry a paragraph stating that contact-upload is DELIBERATELY not a
call site, on the recorded reasoning (REVIEW-57-L5) that it is "a single-shot upload the
operator watches in real time". Autonomy is exactly the condition under which that premise
stops holding, so the paragraph must be corrected in the same phase that breaks it — not left
to contradict the code.

Output: a caller-minted run handle, two `record_audit` calls and one `build_run_report` call in
`contact-upload`, one `build_run_report` call in `suggest-contacts`, two corrected paragraphs in
the analog skills, the sequence-registry entry the contact-upload change requires, and a new
call-site pin.
