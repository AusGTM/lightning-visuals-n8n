Stop markers reaching the caller, and record the one live observation nobody could explain in the
place a future agent will look for it.

items were marker-shaped — a full outcome projection stamped onto an item that carried no row
identity at all. Plan 70-13 removes the executions that produced half of them; this plan removes
the leak itself, so a phantom row cannot be reported as a result on either lane. And execution
12316 showed three nodes running with a runData source naming a node that has no connection to
them; that observation belongs in the fidelity suite as a divergence the walker does not model,
not as a mechanism the walker guesses at.

Output: a positive row-identity filter at both response builders with a RED-first test built from
the recorded shape, and a frozen reproduction of 12316 that keeps the walker honest about what it
cannot do.
</objective>
