<objective>
Put a real Merge in front of every convergence point in the two remaining multi-inbound workflows,
and make the enrichment webhook answer with an ack only.

Purpose: the tracer proved the mechanism on 29 nodes. This plan applies it to the 123-node
enrichment graph and the 26-node review-decision graph — 20 convergence points, none of which is
a native Merge today — and closes the second result channel on the lane that carries the
operator's real batches.

Output: enrichment and review-decision graphs whose converged nodes run once over all rows, an
enrichment webhook that answers `{run_id, accepted, row_ids}` and nothing else, and refusal
reasons that reach the client as rows.
</objective>
