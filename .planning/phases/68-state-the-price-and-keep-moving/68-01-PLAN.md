<objective>
Build the one genuinely new mechanism this phase needs — a real, testable, once-per-round pre-spend
pause — and pin the boundary that keeps the implicit-approval posture out of the headless lane.

Purpose: D-68-01 makes approval implicit. The only thing that makes "the operator can interrupt"
true rather than decorative is a window that actually exists (D-68-05). That window has to survive
two hard constraints at once: the no-sleep/no-while/no-time-import ratchet over every plugin script
except `watch.py`, and the Claude Code harness's refusal of a foreground shell `sleep`. And because
D-68-04 records the unattended intent while deliberately leaving the gate to Phase 67, the new
posture must be provably unreachable from `scheduled_arm.py` — proved by a test, not by absence.

Output: `watch.PRE_SPEND_PAUSE_SECONDS` + `watch.pre_spend_pause`, their tests, the pause's one
invocation site in `suggest-contacts/SKILL.md` step 4, a structural test pinning `scheduled_arm.py`
grant-free, and one recorded sentence in `backend-control/SKILL.md` stating that the scheduled and
cron paths are unchanged by this phase.
</objective>
