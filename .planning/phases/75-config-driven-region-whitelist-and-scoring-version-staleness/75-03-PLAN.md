<objective>
Stamp the rubric version on every company Decide touches, route a version-stale record back into
Decide at zero cost, and give recompute writes their own standing authority.

Purpose: a rubric change today obliges re-scoring the entire population because nothing records
which version scored a record. This plan makes `lv_icp_scoring_version != config.version` the
definition of stale, gives that staleness a route to the one node that recomputes the veto, and
gives that route a write authority independent of the session-scoped allowlist the operator
must otherwise open by hand.

Output: the fetch-list additions, the `ALLOW_HUBSPOT_RECOMPUTE_WRITES` flag and its
`_writeSafetyAllows` branch, the `ENRICH_CO_GATE` reroute, the SJ-2 backstop, and two new
offline routing tests.

**Spec-less probe fallback skipped: phase has no requirement IDs to probe (visible skip).**
</objective>
