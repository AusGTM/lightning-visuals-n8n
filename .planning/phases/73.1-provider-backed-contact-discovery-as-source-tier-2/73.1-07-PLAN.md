<objective>
Build the sixth cloud workflow: a read-only provider discovery lane that takes one POST per round
carrying every eligible company with a `gap` flag, searches the gap companies through the
ZoomInfo then Apollo then Lusha waterfall with a two-rung role filter, and echoes every company
back with its `num_associated_contacts` and whatever people it found (D-06 through D-11b).

Purpose: this is the phase's headline and the only genuinely new engineering in it. Six of seven
companies in the 2026-09-18 pickleball round had no team page, a cross-host 301 or a coming-soon
site; the website ladder cannot reach them and a provider can.

Output: a new generated workflow with zero write nodes and zero self-dispatch, a `discoverySearch.js`
module holding the three `[ASSUMED]` request shapes behind offline fixtures, a walker test, and
the tooling registration a sixth workflow needs.
</objective>
