## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter for the full list. The two most consequential:

1. **No step renumbering.** The plan's own action text asked for a "new step" between the ladder and filter/synthesise; a pre-existing cross-skill test (`test_suggest_contacts_step_numbering_is_unchanged`) pins step 9 as the last step, and other tests pin the report step number and its anchor text. Inserted the discovery content as a subsection at the tail of the existing step 5 instead — satisfying D-10's reading-order requirement without a renumbering cascade across the file's own pinned line numbers and anchors.
2. **Genuine composition tests over `GRANDFATHERED_UNCOVERED`.** The sequence-coverage ratchet (`test_skill_sequence_coverage.py`) is shrink-only at `MAX_GRANDFATHERED = 0` — three new SKILL.md call sequences (the discovery POST, the discovery merge, the widen recipe) had to be either proven by a real end-to-end test or excluded as `NOT_A_PIPELINE` with a genuine no-result-flows reason. All three genuinely DO have results flowing between their calls, so all three got real composition tests rather than a stretched exclusion.
