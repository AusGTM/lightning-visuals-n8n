## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter — summarized: (1) the ingest lane's new rungs
feed the existing `resolveIdentity()` resolver rather than `matchProposal.js`'s
`laneOf`/`summarizeMatch`, keeping one identity authority; (2) the Milestone-2
hard-safety rule is *amended*, not removed, scoped precisely to
`linkedin_url`/`mobilephone`/`phone`; (3) `phone` gets no seed in `Decide Action` —
it flows through `MERGE_CONTACTS`' existing non-clobber engine, which already promotes
it correctly; (4) Test 6 asserts at `Decide Action`'s proposed payload rather than
requiring an armed `HubSpot Create`, because a genuinely emailless create currently has
no allowlist key to arm it by — a pre-existing gap this plan exposes but does not own;
(5) the portal check is a new field on the existing status response, a pure comparator
function, and `init_check.py` (network-free by design) reports only whether it is armed.
