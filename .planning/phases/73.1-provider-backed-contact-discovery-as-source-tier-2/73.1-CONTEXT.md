# Phase 73.1: Provider-backed contact discovery as source tier 2 - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-18
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

suggest-contacts discovers people through the enrichment providers (ZoomInfo, Apollo, Lusha
people/contact search), not only by crawling the company's own website. Operator ruling
2026-09-18 after the pickleball round (people found at 1 of 7 companies): **provider discovery
is source tier 2; LinkedIn moves to tier 3; industry bodies and media move to tier 4.** Tier 1
(the company's own host) is unchanged.

Delivers: the re-tiered allowlist and its constants; a NEW read-only n8n discovery workflow
(6th cloud workflow, own webhook, built by `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py`, zero
self-dispatch per D-70-24); the plugin side of the round that calls it as a gap-fill after the
website ladder; provenance/dedupe for provider-found people; role-vocabulary families the
pickleball round dropped; cost disclosure for discovery; and four UAT-folded fixes from the
2026-09-18 pickleball sitting — portal pin, session-wide grant, connector writes under the
grant, and an ingest identity ladder beyond email.

Out of scope (roadmap, unchanged): crawling LinkedIn (ToS); following a 301 to a different
host (D-62-03 refusal stands); changing the email-domain partition rule's own logic.

Two roadmap lines are OVERRIDDEN by operator rulings in this discussion and the planner must
treat the rulings as authoritative: (a) "identity ladder unchanged" — D-14/D-15 widen it;
(b) "a round states what discovery will cost before spending" — D-12 lets an unknown-rate
provider proceed and report after.

## Implementation Decisions

### Sendability under the new numbering
- **D-01:** Ranks **1 and 2 are sendable; 3 and 4 are held.** `STRONG_TIERS` becomes `(1, 2)`
  with tier 2 now meaning providers; LinkedIn (now 3) flips from sendable to held vs today;
  industry (now 4) stays held. Tier 5 (or a non-numeric sentinel — Claude's call) is the
  absence of a match; `LISTED_TIERS` becomes `(3, 4)`; tier 2 is STAMPED by the discovery
  adapter, never matched by host in `_tier_of`. — **Reversibility:** reversible — constants
  + allowlist + a parity test.
- **D-02:** `partition_for_dispatch`'s email-domain relation is unchanged for a provider-found
  person: after stage 2 reveals an email, a personal or unrelated domain is held exactly as
  today. (See D-16 for the no-email case.)
- **D-03:** Dedupe "one person, not two": a person found on the company page (rank 1) AND by
  a provider (rank 2) is ONE row stamped with the **lowest rank's** provenance and locator; the
  other hit is recorded as corroboration inside the provenance entry. Dedupe key:
  `linkedin_url` when both carry one, else casefolded `firstname+lastname+company`.
- **D-04:** The row's `jobtitle` is the **winning source's** title; the other source's title is
  kept in provenance, never on the row.
- **D-05:** `hold_weak_sources` currently passes through any record whose `provenance.input`
  is not `suggest_contacts_web_search` (fail-open). Provider-discovered records MUST NOT bypass
  the tier gate: either the gate widens to recognise the provider input value, or provider
  records reuse a gated input with `source_tier: 2`. Planner picks; a test pins that a rank-2
  record with an unrelated email is still held and a rank-3/4 record is never sendable.
- **D-05a:** The parity test pins ALL FOUR tier constants against the allowlist:
  `STRONG_TIERS`, `KNOWN_TIERS`, `LISTED_TIERS` (`search_fallback.py`) and
  `SEARCH_SOURCE_TIERS` (`suggest_contacts.py`, currently `(1, 2, 3)`).

### Lane shape and response
- **D-06:** Discovery is a **new 6th cloud workflow with its own webhook path** (e.g.
  `hubspot/suggest/discover`). Read-only: no HubSpot writes, so no write gate, no arming
  window; provider credentials reused from n8n. Built and regenerated by
  `scripts/build_cloud_workflows.py`; `assert_no_self_dispatch` stays in force. Whether it
  gets a local replica is Claude's discretion (the convention is mixed: enrichment and ingest
  have one, backend-status/review/maintenance do not). — **Reversibility:** costly — a 6th workflow needs deploy,
  bounce, and its own execution accounting; folding it into the enrichment lane later would
  be a re-splice.
- **D-07:** Response channel is **hybrid**: the webhook answers synchronously with a data body
  `{run_id, companies: [{company_id, num_associated_contacts, people: [...]}]}`; on timeout or a
  partial body the plugin recovers the same people from settled runData keyed on the
  client-minted `run_id` (`run_state.new_run_id()`, `watch.recover_async_dispatch` idiom). The
  body is the fast path, runData the durable path. `run_id` rides in the request either way.
- **D-08:** **One POST per round** carrying **every eligible company** with a `gap` boolean:
  body `{run_id, companies: [{company_id, name, domain, role_families,
  num_associated_contacts, gap}]}`; the lane **searches only `gap: true`** rows (D-10) and
  echoes every row. Response keyed by `company_id`. One n8n execution per round.
- **D-09 (folded todo):** The plugin sends `num_associated_contacts` (it already has it from
  the enrichment lane's anonymous response) and the lane **echoes it beside `company_id`** for
  ALL eligible companies — gap and non-gap — in both body and runData, so an audit
  reconstructs the full eligible set from one execution. Non-gap rows cost no provider call.
  No HubSpot re-read inside the lane.

### When discovery runs
- **D-10:** **Website ladder first; providers fill the gap.** Providers are queried only for
  companies where the ladder found fewer than `per_company_cap` people. One POST for the gap
  set. (Pickleball round: 6 of 7 would have gone to providers; PA would not.)
- **D-11:** Inside the lane, per company, the waterfall is **ZoomInfo > Apollo > Lusha, stop
  when the cap is met** — the existing convention (`enrichment.FULL_WATERFALL`,
  `config/provider_priority.yaml` default). Not the roadmap's Apollo-first order. At most one
  search per provider per company; `MAX_FALLBACK_SEARCHES = 3` is reused as the per-company
  ceiling (three providers, one search each).
- **D-11a:** Role filtering is a two-rung ladder: **rung 1 = provider-side title filter** built
  from the chosen role families' member strings (e.g. Apollo `person_titles`); **rung 2, only
  when rung 1 returns zero** = unfiltered top-10 from that provider, classified locally. Result
  cap **10 per provider per company** before classification. Operator judged "both, always"
  too conservative.
- **D-11b:** `role_classify.classify_title` (contiguous-token, longest-wins, never fuzzy)
  **still gates every rung-1 result** and stamps `role_family`; a title it cannot place is
  dropped `role_not_selected` as today. Trusting the provider's match instead is NOT shipped:
  the end-of-phase live proof measures a small sample (provider-filter hit vs classifier
  verdict) and the operator rules on that evidence afterwards.

  **RULED 2026-09-19 (verify-work Test 2, executions `12671` + `12675`, 9 live rung-1
  hits):** the classifier gate STAYS and does not relax for ZoomInfo-search-sourced
  candidates. ZoomInfo's `jobTitle` filter is substring-loose — under a CEO filter it
  returned `Executive Assistant To Chief Executive Officer` and `Assistant To the Chief
  Executive Officer` (2 of 4 AFL hits); longest-wins alone classified the second as the
  CEO's own family. The ruling adds ONE negative token, `assistant`: a title carrying it
  vetoes every member that does not itself name it (`role_classify.NEGATIVE_TOKENS`,
  `classify_title`), so `Executive Assistant` still lands in Administration and the two
  assistants above drop `role_not_selected`. Replayed over execution `12675`'s real people:
  tennis.com.au 5 kept / 0 dropped, afl.com.au 2 kept / 2 dropped. Plugin-side only — the
  n8n lane returns raw people and is unchanged.
- **D-11c:** Role vocabulary gains the families the pickleball round dropped —
  `executive_officer` (Executive Officer ≈ CEO) and `board_chair` (Board Chair / Chairwoman /
  Chairman ≈ President) — in `role_vocabulary.yaml` under the same `evidenced: false` /
  `generic_fallback` discipline (D-62-07 stays AMENDED); the bare-grade-noun structural test
  is unchanged.

### Cost and what spends
- **D-12 (operator ruling — deferred to evidence):** Whether the discovery lane is
  **search-only** (0 credits, email/phone reveal stays in stage 2's existing enrich path) or
  may **reveal in-lane** is NOT decided here. A **plan-time live probe** (one search call per
  provider against a known company, provider balance read before/after via the endpoints in
  memory `provider-credit-check-endpoints`; Apollo's balance is unreadable — 403 — so its
  search is priced from vendor documentation only) returns the per-provider answer to the
  operator, who rules. The probe runs in the RESEARCH step so the ruling lands before PLAN.md
  is final (the 73-CONTEXT "plan-time rulings after research" pattern). **Assumption (mine,
  not the operator's):** until ruled, the planner designs for search-only.
- **D-13:** A provider whose search rate is unknown (`null`) at round time **proceeds and
  reports the measured cost after** — overriding the roadmap's "states cost before spending"
  for that provider only. Known rates are disclosed up front; unknown is rendered as unknown,
  never as zero (existing `cost_guard` convention). `cost_rates.json` gains one search-rate key
  per provider (`null` until measured, dated, cited).
- **D-13a:** Disclosure line shape is Claude's discretion (see below); it must show gap-company
  count, per-provider search rate or "unknown", the 1 n8n execution, and the stage-2 reveal
  ceiling at the contact rate × (cap × gap companies).

### Portal pin (UAT fold)
- **D-14a:** Any HubSpot tool outside the plugin lanes (claude.ai HubSpot connector, MCP, `hs`
  CLI) may be used — **reads and writes — only after THAT TOOL proves it is on portal
  `22617666`**; a mismatch stops the step and says so. Proof for the outside tool: before its
  first call, look up through it a record the plugin knows exists (an `hs_object_id` from
  `written_records`, or a configured sentinel id); zero results = wrong portal, stop. The UAT
  session's connector was on a different portal and returned zero results for records the
  plugin had just written. D-14a/D-14b are **skill-text rules enforced by the grep test
  (D-14d), not runtime code gates** — the planner must not invent a hook.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — a shared skill rule + test.
- **D-14b:** A connector write on the matching portal is **under the same session grant as
  every other write path**: `write_grant.covers()` is checked before the write; the outcome is
  appended to `written_records` with `source: connector`; a created contact is associated to
  its company in the same step (the ingest lane's rule, §13.0.1). One yes governs all paths.
- **D-14c:** Backend-side portal proof is live: `wf_backend_status_cloud` reads the HubSpot
  account (e.g. `GET /account-info/v3/details` — researcher verifies endpoint and scope) with
  the n8n credential and returns `portalId`; this proves the n8n credential's portal, and
  D-14a's lookup proves the outside tool's;
  `initialize` and `backend-status` compare it to `hubspot_portal_id`. `hubspot_portal_id`
  stays **optional** in `operator.local.json`; the guard runs only when it is set.
- **D-14d:** The CRM-access rule lives in **one shared skill block** included by every skill,
  pinned by a test that greps each `SKILL.md` for it.

### Session grant (UAT fold)
- **D-15a:** The **first yes covers the whole session**: all lanes, the domains/ids named in
  that proposal. Every later send goes through `covers()` on that grant. The UAT session opened
  three grants (`session batch`, `PA landing`, `PA ingest`) where the first covered everything.
- **D-15b:** A send on a domain/id the open grant does not cover **auto-widens the grant and
  states it** to the operator — no question, never a second grant.
- **D-15c:** A second `open_grant` while one is open is **refused, naming the open grant**
  ("grant `<label>` already open; use `covers()` or widen"). Structural stop for the
  triple-open.
- **D-15d:** The ask states scope and revocation in one sentence, from the shared block:
  "This yes covers every send this session across lanes X for domains Y, widening as new
  domains appear; say revoke at any time." `CLOSED_REVOKED` is the existing revoke path.

### Emailless create (UAT fold — identity ladder in the ingest lane)
- **D-16a:** The contact ingest lane gains an **identity ladder** for MATCHING: `email` >
  `linkedin_url` (`hs_linkedin_url` EQ) > `mobilephone` > other. A **probe in the research
  step** of HubSpot contact search on each candidate property decides which rungs exist
  (which properties are searchable with EQ on this portal); the operator rules on the result
  before PLAN.md is final. The LinkedIn rung reuses the enrichment lane's `IF Linkedin
  Searchable` → `HubSpot Linkedin Search` → `Adapt Linkedin Search` idiom (Phase 61),
  regenerated by the builder. Match → update; no match → create.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — new search hops + carry Merges + sentinels under the Phase 70
  idiom on the ingest graph.
- **D-16a-i (operator ruling):** **`phone` is identity for CREATE only, never a match rung.**
  A landline is often a company switchboard shared by every contact there; EQ on it would
  update the wrong person (Liam's provider row carried a stray `+1 442-819-2966`).
  **`mobilephone` matches on EQ only when exactly ONE hit**; more than one hit → `review`,
  never update. The same single-hit rule applies to the LinkedIn rung.
- **D-16b:** **Any row with `linkedin_url` OR `phone` OR `mobilephone`** (and no email) is
  sendable. `extraction.hold_emailless` relaxes to hold only rows with none of
  email/linkedin_url/mobilephone/phone. `required_identity.any_of` in
  `config/column_mapping.yaml` / `n8n/code/columnMap.js` / the plugin's shipped copy gains
  **name + number groups — `[firstname, lastname, mobilephone]` and
  `[firstname, lastname, phone]`** (operator ruling; a bare number is NOT identity, so a CSV
  row carrying only a phone still fails identity); parity test updated. Overrides the
  roadmap's "identity ladder unchanged".
- **D-16c:** The email-domain relation in `partition_for_dispatch` applies **only when an
  email exists**; an emailless LinkedIn/phone-keyed row is not held `company_domain_unknown`.
- **D-16d:** A created emailless contact lands like any other: associated to its company, one
  `written_records` entry, one report row showing which identity key resolved it.
