24 updates + 11 creates + 1 skip (ground truth: runData, executions `12432`–`12449`, run
`6891d018e84f4d869eb8080292dac6c5`); the plugin reported 11 creates + 1 skip and 19 unjoinable
research rows, and could not see a single update.

it go RED, and only then find and fix the loss. Research (Open Question 3) established that the
join rule D-73-11 describes — `row_id` then `hs_object_id` — is ALREADY implemented in
`_identity_for_entry`; this plan is a diagnosis, not a new join.

Purpose: an operator who cannot see 24 of 36 outcomes cannot trust any report this tool produces.
Output: a redacted recording that pins the behaviour permanently, plus the fix it forces.
</objective>
