<objective>
The companies branch matched `domain EQ <bare>` and so missed every portal record HubSpot stores
as `www.<bare>`: attempt 2 duplicated Racing Victoria, Wyong and Canberra RC that way — three of
eleven creates, on top of Perth Racing's separate name-plus-TLD case. A name-only row (Illawarra)
sent `domain EQ ""`, took a 400, was mislabelled as a failed lookup, and so never reached the
name fallback that could have matched it. And a company whose website was a freemail address was
accepted and created.

All three are one lane's identity step. Fix them together: one IN search over a
`[bare, www.bare]` pair that can never be empty, honest name-only outcomes, and a freemail
refusal in both engines.

Purpose: Stage B of attempt 3 must create only genuinely new companies. Output: a companies
branch whose match is as wide as the portal's own storage forms and whose refusals say what to do.
</objective>
