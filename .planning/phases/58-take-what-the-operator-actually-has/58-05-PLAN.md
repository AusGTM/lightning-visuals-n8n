## What this plan closes, and what authorises it

**Gap (operator walk, 2026-08-26, dispositioned by operator ruling — recorded in
`58-03-SUMMARY.md` § Decisions Made, item 3):** native record-page fields stay blank on a company
this lane lands.

**Root cause, traced to source before planning — three distinct causes, of which this plan fixes
the first and third and deliberately leaves the second alone:**

1. `country`, `city` and `numberofemployees` have **no candidate at all**. The three company
   branches of `n8n/code/normalizeProviders.js` push only `lv_revenue_band`, `lv_employee_band`,
   `industry` and `lv_country_region_normalized` (Lusha ~lines 314-329, Apollo ~lines 396-403,
   ZoomInfo ~lines 486-505). The raw values these native fields need are already in scope at
   those exact lines and are simply consumed for the `lv_*` derivation and then discarded.
   `config/field_policy.yaml` already carries a `numberofemployees` entry — it has been dead
   policy, because no candidate ever arrives to be judged by it.
2. `industry` DOES have a candidate and IS policy-eligible (`stale_refreshable`/75 in both
   engines). It is refused by the Phase 31 enum guard in `mergeCompanies.js`, which sets
   `decision = "stage_only"` and `validation_status = "rejected"` for any value that is not an
   exact case-insensitive label match. This is designed behaviour working exactly as specified,
   not a bug — and the operator upheld it on 2026-08-26. **This plan does not touch that guard,
   that module, or that outcome.**
3. `country` and `city` are absent from `ENRICH_COMPANY_SEARCH_PROPERTIES_CSV`, so even once
   candidates exist the `existingRecord` would carry no current value for the non-clobber branch
   to compare against.

**One finding that shrinks this plan, verified from source before planning — do not re-derive it,
but do pin it:**

- **There is no write-map allowlist on the companies branch.** `ENRICH_DECIDE_CO_CLOUD` builds its
  patch as `properties = { ...merge.canonicalPatch, ...(merge.cacheKeys || {}), ...(row.lusha_ids
  || {}) }` — a wholesale spread. Any field `mergeCompanies` promotes ships. Nothing in the write
  map needs editing; the correct move is to VERIFY that and pin the verification, exactly as
  `58-04` Task 3 instructs for `validateResearchOutput`'s wholesale spread.

The Phase 46 parity rule binds the two places that hold one shared contract:
`config/field_policy.yaml`'s companies section and `mergeCompanies.js`'s `DEFAULT_COMPANY_POLICY`.
