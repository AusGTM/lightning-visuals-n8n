<objective>
Build the lane that turns a proposed domain into a decided one: a batch table the operator can
approve wholesale or correct row by row, and a decision-application function that refuses
everything when any one entry is bad.

Purpose: INPUT-03 — a researched domain is confirmed before it is written. Getting this wrong
creates a company under the wrong domain, which is the dedupe anchor for everything after it.
INPUT-04 — every refusal on this lane names what would make it work.

Output: `company_domain.py`, its tests, the envelope's consumption of decided rows, and the
operator-facing confirm step in the two skills that reach the company lane.
</objective>
