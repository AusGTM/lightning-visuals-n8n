<objective>
Give the system the confidence self-assessment whose absence IS finding F, and turn it into the
autonomy policy D-61-07 locks: confident rows proceed unattended, unconfident rows are held
with a reason, and the batch always finishes.

Purpose: the operator's diagnosis is that there is no confidence self-assessment and therefore
no autonomy — 300 contacts become 300 conversations. D-61-07 makes deciding what confidence
MEANS core phase work, not a detail. This plan decides it, from signals the pipeline already
produces, and builds the hold-and-collect it hangs on.

Output: a deterministic confidence verdict per row, a durable held-rows queue that survives the
session, and a batch that reaches its last row regardless of what any single row does.

What is NOT relaxed here: the non-clobber merge policy, the n8n write-safety gate nodes,
`plan_grant`'s empty-record-set refusal, the material-conflict judge gate, per-send armed-window
narrowing, and the post-run written-records account. The operator kept non-clobbering by name.
What IS removed is approval friction and per-row research halts.
</objective>
