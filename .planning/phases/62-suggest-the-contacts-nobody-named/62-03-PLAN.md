  <context>
D-62-11 is rated **one-way**, and Task 3 is what makes it real.

**Why one-way:** the envelope's contents are what the operator agreed to. Once operators have
learned that "one grant covers this session" includes suggestions, narrowing it later breaks the
consent model Phase 53 established (D-53-05/D-53-06, proved live in walk run 3) and would require
re-walking GRANT-01.

**What CONTEXT.md already records:** the operator overruled an earlier framing that proposed a
separate confirmation on the grounds that grants authorise writes while discovery only spends
credit — *"a single grant covers the entire session (this would include suggestions)."* This
checkpoint exists because the decision is one-way, not because the answer is unknown.

**What the chosen option costs in practice, so the yes is informed:**
- The grant's disclosed number gets BIGGER. Every batch that opens a grant shows a suggestion
  allowance whether or not the operator later runs a round.
- The allowance is priced worst case, treating EVERY company in the batch as eligible for the full
  per-company cap, because eligibility is unknowable at grant time (the grant opens before the
  batch runs). It over-states by design, which is the safe direction for a budget guard.
- A batch that was previously under the monthly ceiling may now be refused as `CEILING_OVER`,
  carrying Phase 57's split offer.
  </context>
