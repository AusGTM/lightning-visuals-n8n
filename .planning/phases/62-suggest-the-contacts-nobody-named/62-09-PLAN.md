## Decision 1 — the matching rule: CONTIGUOUS TOKEN RUN, longest wins, never substring

Both sides are normalised, then tokenised, then a member matches a title when the member's token
sequence appears as a **contiguous run of whole tokens** inside the title's tokens.

Normalisation, in this order, applied identically to the member and the title:
`html.unescape` (the portal stores `AV &amp; Broadcast Senior Executive` with the entity
unescaped) -> casefold -> `&` becomes the word `and` -> every remaining non-alphanumeric
character becomes a space -> split on whitespace. So `Finance & Admin Officer` and
`Finance and Admin Officer` are the same token list, and `Secretary/Manager` tokenises as two
tokens rather than one.

Why a token run and not a substring: substring matching makes `Manager` match `Track Manager`,
`Catering Manager` and everything else with the word in it — the exact over-match the operator
called out. Why contiguous and not bag-of-tokens overlap: overlap makes `General Manager` match
`Track Manager` on the shared word `manager`, which is the named negative. A contiguous run of
`general manager` simply does not occur in `track manager`, so that match is unreachable rather
than merely unlikely.

**Longest wins.** Among every (family, member) pair that matches, the one with the most tokens is
chosen; ties break on family order then member order, so the result is deterministic. Without
this, a `President` family and a `Vice President` family both match "Vice President" and the
answer depends on YAML ordering. An exact whole-title match is simply the longest possible run
and needs no special case.

Unchanged: a title matching nothing is `None`, never a guess (`classify_title`'s standing
contract). The family list stays a parameter — this module still never derives or refreshes it.

## Decision 2 — a single-token member may be a role NOUN, never a grade noun

`Director`, `Chairman`, `President`, `Treasurer`, `Secretary` are meaningful alone and are
allowed as one-token members. `Manager`, `Officer`, `Executive`, `Assistant`, `Coordinator`,
`Supervisor`, `Head`, `Lead` are grade nouns that appear in most job titles and are FORBIDDEN as
bare members — one of them alone would make its family match nearly every person on a board page
and quietly spend the round's cap on people the operator did not choose.

This is enforced structurally, not by care: a test walks the shipped vocabulary and fails on any
single-token member in the forbidden set. That guard is the durable half of this decision — the
YAML will be edited again, and the rule has to outlive whoever edits it.

Accepted looseness, stated rather than discovered: a one-token `Director` member also matches
`Director of Catering`. That is the cost of honouring the operator's partial-match ruling with a
rule simple enough to reason about, and it errs toward offering a person the operator can decline
rather than dropping one they wanted. The cap (D-62-12) still bounds what any of it can spend.

## Decision 3 — the existing eight families are kept verbatim; the governance vocabulary is ADDED

Every existing label and member stays exactly as it is. `test_suggest_contacts_composition.py`
pins `Head of Broadcast` by name, and this phase's own live evidence is that
`Operations Manager` and `General Manager` matched real people. Additive change only; the
disclosure metadata (`evidenced: false`, `source: generic_fallback`) is untouched because it is
still true.

`top_n` becomes the new family count — it is a document-level metadata field the loader's own
test requires to be present, and for an un-evidenced list where every `recurrence` is 0 it
describes the menu length, not a ranking. The menu grows to roughly seventeen lines; that is
acceptable for a fallback the operator scans once per batch, and no pagination is added.

## Decision 4 — the matcher changes in ONE place: `role_classify.classify_title`

`select_people` already delegates every role question to it and is not touched. The derivation
script `scripts/role_vocabulary.py` is not touched either — it would have to emit families under
the same rule if it ever ran, but it does not run today (G-62-5) and speculatively editing a
crashing script that this round never calls buys nothing.
