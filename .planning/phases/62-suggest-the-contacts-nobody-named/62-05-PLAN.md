<objective>
The sitting itself: an operator-attended round that reaches a company's people page through the
sitemap ladder, filters to the roles chosen once for the batch, enriches the people it named, and
lands them as proposals.

Purpose: this is where SUGGEST-01, -02, -04 and -05 become operator-visible. Everything the round
does is assembled from modules the earlier plans built and from machinery
`enrich-before-ingest/SKILL.md` already uses — the skill composes, it does not re-implement.

**Where the round runs:** in the plugin, as an operator-attended sitting (D-62-01 rev 3). `web_fetch`
exists only on the conversational side; the backend has `web_search` alone. There is no backend
fetching in this plan, no schedule, and no unattended path.

Output: the new skill, the composition test the sequence ratchet requires, the unprompted offer in
the skill that completes a company batch, and the release bump.

Implements D-62-01 (ladder-sourced discovery, plugin-side), D-62-02 (two stages, one price, no
confirmation between them), D-62-10 (the whole round lands as proposals), D-62-11 (one grant covers
it), D-62-12 (per-company cap chosen once), D-62-15 (auto-offered after a batch completes).
</objective>
