<objective>
Name the CAUSE of a round that ends with nothing usable, in one pure classifier, and route on
it — including routing to "spend nothing", which is the right answer for three of the six
outcomes.

Purpose: today `skills/suggest-contacts/SKILL.md` decides the round's next move from an inline
`if not people:` — a LADDER-empty test standing in for a ROUND-empty question. Two live rounds
(Brisbane Roar, The Roma Turf Club) ended with zero sendable people and neither would have been
rescued by a retry, because their causes were a matcher failure and an email failure. This plan
moves the decision out of prose into `round_outcome`, where a test can pin it (D-65-01).

Output: `suggest_contacts.round_outcome` plus its two closed vocabularies, the two documented
call sites, the per-company cause/breakdown on the round's own output structure, and the tests
that pin the precedence, the fail-closed behaviour, and the three hard constraints (SAFE-02,
SAFE-03, no `while`).
</objective>
