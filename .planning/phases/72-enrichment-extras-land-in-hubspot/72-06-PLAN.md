<objective>
Give companies the same geo shape contacts already have, plus a phone slot, and only for
fields a real provider response actually carries.

Purpose: `n8n/code/normalizeProviders.js`'s company branches produce exactly two location
signals today — `country` and `city`, added by 58-05 — while the contacts branches of the same
file produce `city`, `state`, `country` and both ISO-code fields for all three providers. The
company side has no `state` producer, no code producers, no `phone` producer and no second
domain producer. D-72-14/15 close that, and the discipline that matters is the one the
existing company-`domain` gap already demonstrates: a policy entry with `promote_to_canonical:
true` and zero reachable producers is permanently unreachable dead configuration, and shipping
one is worse than recording an honest gap.

So this plan reads the provider responses first. Where Lusha's `co.location` or Apollo's `org`
record demonstrably carries a state or a phone — the 58-05 comments already cite live
executions for `country` and `city` from the same objects — a producer is written. Where none
of the three carries it, the field is recorded as an accepted no-producer gap with a pending
todo, and no policy entry claims otherwise.

D-72-16 is the boundary this plan must not cross. Contacts now carry geo through both lanes,
and `lv_country_region_normalized` is an ICP scoring input behind the 58-06 judge gate. A
person's city is not evidence of their employer's region, and nothing here may connect the two.

Output: new company producers backed by named evidence, matching policy entries, a widened
`Merge Company` candidate loop, and an explicit gap list for anything no provider supplies.
</objective>
