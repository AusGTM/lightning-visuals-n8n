<objective>
Build the June-2026 validation dataset into a reproducible, committed candidate table and wire it
into the live n8n company enrichment lane as a third merge candidate source, so that when the
pipeline runs against the 66 real companies it has June's ICP signals available, adjudicates them
against fresh research instead of clobbering, and fills the two firmographic bands the
zero-provider research lane structurally cannot supply.

This is the offline half of the phase's single end-to-end path. Nothing here touches the live
portal or spends a credit; it exists solely to make Plan 03's live canary tracer runnable.

Purpose: DATA-01 requires the 66 companies to land with `lv_*` inputs and provenance stamped. The
research lane alone cannot deliver `lv_revenue_band` (which is worth up to +10 or -50 in
`config/icp_scoring.yaml`) and cannot compare its answers against the June analysis. Both gaps
close here, in the pipeline, before any record is written.

Output: `config/june_candidates.json` (66 mapped rows), a committed source snapshot, three new
folds in the company merge node, and the regenerated cloud workflow JSON.
</objective>

<design_decisions_resolved_here>
These four were left open by the locked decisions. Each is settled below, with rationale, and is
not to be revisited during execution.

**F1 — where `lv_revenue_band` / `lv_employee_band` come from.** RESOLVED: option (a), derive both
deterministically from the record's own `annualrevenue` / `numberofemployees` natives, with option
(c) as the documented residue. Measured against the June portal export of the same 66 companies:
`annualrevenue` is populated on 60/66 and `numberofemployees` on 61/66. Banding those natives with
`src/normalizer.py`'s cut points puts 32 of the 66 into `5-50M` or `50-500M` (+10 each), 24 into
`1-5M`, 2 into `<1M`, 1 into `1B-1.2B` (-30) and 1 into `1.2B+` (-50). Without this fold those 32
records score 10 low and several land a tier below where the rubric puts them. Option (b) — adding
the two fields to the web-research prompt — is rejected: it costs more Anthropic per record and
research-inferred revenue is weaker data than the firmographic already sitting on the record. The
6 records with no `annualrevenue` and the 5 with no `numberofemployees` get no band; they are
listed in the Plan 04 run report as a known score-impact gap, not silently absorbed.

June's own `employee_estimate` is deliberately NOT used. Its ranges (`200-500`, `500+`, `50-150`)
do not align with the live `lv_employee_band` cut points, it would need an invented rounding rule,
it covers only 5 records the natives miss, and `lv_employee_band` does not feed the ICP score at
all (absent from `config/icp_scoring.yaml`'s `base_score` block).

**F2 — how D-04's conflict routing actually reaches `needs_review`.** RESOLVED, and the planning
context's proposed option (a) is rejected on direct evidence: extending `CONFLICT_WATCH` would be
a silent no-op on the live write path. `CONFLICT_WATCH` produces `row.conflicts`, which is read by
`ENRICH_DECIDE_CO_LOCAL` (the dry-run echo node) and by nothing else. `ENRICH_DECIDE_CO_CLOUD` —
the node that actually builds the HubSpot PATCH — derives review state solely from
`(merge.decisions || []).filter((d) => d.decision === "needs_review")`. Therefore D-04 is
implemented by emitting a synthetic `needs_review` **decision** into the merged `decisions` array
and removing the disputed field from `canonicalPatch`, which plugs into the existing consumer with
zero changes to the decide node. Task 3 owns this.

**F4 — where execution stops.** This plan is fully autonomous and offline. Every live step
(deploy, resolve, arm, queue, disarm, sweep) is an operator checkpoint in Plans 03 and 04.

**Reproducibility of the sibling dataset.** The source lives outside this git root at
`../ausgtm-lightningvisuals-data/data/enriched_companies.json` and cannot be depended on
silently. Task 1 commits a verbatim snapshot as `config/june_candidates_source.json` (41 KB) plus
a sha256 of it inside the generated table's header, so the mapping is reproducible from this repo
alone and any future divergence is detectable.
</design_decisions_resolved_here>
