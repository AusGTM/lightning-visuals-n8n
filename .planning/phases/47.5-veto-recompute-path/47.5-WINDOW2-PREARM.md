## The one divergence to understand before arming

For Simtech, the oracle's "before" says vetoed while the LIVE record says not vetoed. That is
**not** a contradiction — it is the pending deploy, visible:

- `src/icp_scoring.py` already fires the OR predicate (wave 4 landed it).
- The deployed `Decide Company Action` node still runs the OLD boolean-only predicate.

So the divergence closes the moment the operator deploys and the workflow is bounced. **If the
window were armed before that deploy, Simtech would recompute under the old rule and stay Tier
B** — the decision would be recorded and silently not applied, which is precisely the failure
class this phase exists to remove. Arming is therefore blocked on the deploy, not merely
sequenced after it.
