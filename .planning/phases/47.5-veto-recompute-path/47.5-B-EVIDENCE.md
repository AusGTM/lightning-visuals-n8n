## The bright line, quoted from D-V6

> **A substantive local operating entity: a subsidiary, office, staff, or production operations in
> Australia or New Zealand.**
>
> Explicitly **not**:
> - selling into the market from overseas;
> - having ANZ customers, viewers, or events;
> - a reseller, distributor, or agent relationship;
> - a domain or landing page targeted at ANZ.

> Research must **cite evidence of the local entity** … Absent that evidence the value is `Unknown`,
> never `Other` by default and never `ANZ` by inference.

Source: `.planning/decisions/2026-08-12-org-type-venue-and-normalization.md` § D-V6.

## Method, and what "verified" means below

Two Anthropic `web_search` calls (one per candidate) posed the operating-presence question with
D-V6's exclusions restated, and were required to return verbatim quotes with URLs. **Every quote a
verdict rests on was then independently re-fetched and grepped by hand.** Quotes marked
**VERIFIED** were found in the live page by direct fetch; quotes marked *search-reported* could not
be re-fetched (Cloudflare challenge / JS-rendered page) and are recorded as corroboration only —
no verdict rests on them.

Primary registry sources (ABN Lookup, NZ Companies Office) were fetched directly. They are the
load-bearing evidence for both verdicts; the companies' own sites are corroboration.

The live enum options are in `47.5-B-BEFORE.json`: `AU · NZ · ANZ · US · UK · EU · Other · Unknown`.

## Ironman — `17317184159` — verdict: **meets**

**Question:** does Ironman (HQ Tampa, US) have a substantive local operating entity in Australia or
New Zealand — a subsidiary, office, staff, or production operations? Specifically, are ANZ
operations run by an **owned** entity or by a licensee/franchisee (which D-V6 excludes)?

### Australia — owned subsidiary, registered since 1991, trading as the group

**https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?abn=67052342239** — **VERIFIED**, fetched directly
2026-08-12:

> "Entity name: USM EVENTS PTY LTD  ABN status: Active from 09 Apr 2000  Entity type: Australian
> Private Company  Goods & Services Tax (GST): Registered from 01 Jul 2000  Main business location:
> VIC 3205"

and, from the registered business names on the same record:

> "The IRONMAN Group 08 Apr 2025 … IRONMAN Australia 20 Sep 2013 … UNITED SPORTS MARKETING 09 Apr 2000"

This is the discriminating fact. An Australian private company holds the registered business names
**"The IRONMAN Group"** and **"IRONMAN Australia"**, is GST-registered, and its main business
location is VIC 3205 — South Melbourne. A licensee running franchised events does not register the
group's own name as its business name while operating as an Australian private company.

### New Zealand — owned subsidiary, group is the ultimate holding company

**https://app.companiesoffice.govt.nz/companies/app/ui/pages/companies/1777952** — **VERIFIED**,
fetched directly 2026-08-12:

> "IRONMAN NEW ZEALAND LIMITED (1777952) Registered … NZBN: 9429034266335 Incorporation Date: 13 Mar
> 2006 Company Status: Registered Entity type: NZ Limited Company"

> "Ultimate holding company **World Endurance Holdings Inc** … Country of registration United States
> of America"

> "Registered Office 347 Parnell Road, Level One, Parnell, Auckland., 1052, New Zealand"

> "Directors … Daniel Scott DERUE 640 Geneva Place, Tampa Florida, FL 33606, United States of
> America … Michelle Marilyn PEPPER 17 Troy Place, Glendowie, Auckland, 1071, New Zealand"

This settles franchise-vs-owned outright, from the registry rather than from marketing copy: the
**declared ultimate holding company is the Ironman parent** (World Endurance Holdings, the Tampa
entity), there is an Auckland registered office, a Tampa-resident director sits on the board, and an
Auckland-resident director sits beside him. That is a subsidiary, not a licence.

### Corroboration (search-reported, not independently re-fetched)

- `https://au.ironmanstore.com/pages/privacy-policy` — *"USM Events Pty Ltd trading as IRONMAN
  Australia ABN 67 052 342 239 of Level 6, 222 Kings Way, South Melbourne, Victoria, 3205,
  Australia"* — the street address behind the registry's "VIC 3205". Self-described; the page is
  JS-rendered and the text could not be re-fetched, so the address is corroboration only. The ABN
  and postcode both match the VERIFIED registry record.
- `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Triathlon_Corporation` — *"In February 2012 WTC purchased USM
  Events, owners of Australian triathlons in Geelong, Mooloolaba, and Noosa"* — an acquisition, not
  a licence. Consistent with the registry; not relied on.
- `https://leadiq.com/c/the-ironman-group/…` — *"Australia · 50 · New Zealand · 15"* headcount. A
  third-party data aggregator; treated as weak, and the verdict does not need it.

### Verdict

**`meets`.** Two registered local operating entities, one in each country, with the group declared
as ultimate holding company in NZ and the group's own business names registered against the AU
entity. Neither is a franchise, a licensee, a distributor, nor a landing page.

**Implied enum value: `ANZ`** — operations span both Australia and New Zealand, and D-V6 reserves
`ANZ` for exactly this shape ("multinational with a substantive ANZ operating presence"). `AU` and
`NZ` both understate it and, per D-V6's table, denote domicile — which is Tampa.

**What would change this verdict:** the NZ record ceasing to show an Ironman-group ultimate holding
company; USM Events' "The IRONMAN Group" / "IRONMAN Australia" business names lapsing or moving to a
third party; evidence that either entity was sold to an independent operator running events under
licence.

## An enum-semantics tension the operator should settle (Gravity Media only)

The two governing texts point at different values for a **foreign-domiciled company with operations
in one ANZ country**:

| Source | Says | Implies for Gravity Media |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-V6's table | `AU` = "domiciled in Australia"; `ANZ` = "multinational with a substantive ANZ operating presence" | **`ANZ`** — it is a UK-domiciled multinational with substantive AU operations |
| This plan's Task 2 wording | "`ANZ` where operations span both countries, `AU` or `NZ` where they do not" | **`AU`** — operations do not span both |

Not resolved here. It is exactly what the checkpoint's `other-value` option exists for. Ironman is
unaffected — both readings give `ANZ`, since its operations do span both countries.

Scoring impact is nil either way: `AU`, `NZ` and `ANZ` all map to the same non-veto `region_key` in
the engine, and geography scores 10 for each.

**The AU-vs-ANZ tension, and how it was resolved.** The evidence file raised a genuine conflict for
Gravity Media: D-V6's table reads `ANZ` (a UK-domiciled multinational with substantive ANZ
operations), while this plan's Task 2 wording reads `AU` (operations do not span both countries).
The operator resolved it **in favour of D-V6's own wording — "ANZ absorbs the
multinational-with-local-operations case."** Both records are foreign-domiciled multinationals with
a substantive local operating entity, which is precisely the case D-V6 created the `ANZ` member for.
The label describes the *pattern*, not a count of countries.

**Reasoning.**

- **Ironman → `ANZ`.** Registry evidence in **both** countries: USM EVENTS PTY LTD (ABN
  67 052 342 239) holding the group's own registered business names, and IRONMAN NEW ZEALAND LIMITED
  (NZ Companies Office 1777952) declaring World Endurance Holdings Inc as ultimate holding company —
  owned, not licensed. `ANZ` is unambiguous here under either reading.
- **Gravity Media → `ANZ`.** Registry evidence is **Australian only**: GRAVITY MEDIA (AUSTRALIA) PTY
  LTD (ABN 90 081 039 401), trading as Gearhouse Broadcast from 2000 and re-registered as Gravity
  Media in October 2019 with the group rebrand. Its NZ result was `insufficient_evidence`. The
  operator accepted `ANZ` anyway, on the ground that the label describes the multinational pattern
  rather than asserting presence in both countries.

> **Recorded so a later reader does not mistake this for two-country evidence: Gravity Media's New
> Zealand leg is UNPROVEN.** `ANZ` on record `15860277364` rests on Australian operating presence
> alone. The only "GRAVITY MEDIA LIMITED" on the NZ Companies Register is an unrelated 2023
> registration at a formation agent's address in Feilding. If NZ presence is ever asserted for this
> record, it needs its own evidence — it is not carried by this decision.

**Still no writes.** This decision authorises plan 06's write set; it does not perform it. The write
leg is wave 5 and stays gated behind workstream A's recompute fix landing — a region change alone
does nothing while `Company Gate` skips a complete record, and all four of these records are in
exactly that state (see `47.5-B-BEFORE.json` → `gate_ttl_note`). That trap is the reason this phase
exists, and writing early would repeat Phase 47's blank-a-field workaround.
