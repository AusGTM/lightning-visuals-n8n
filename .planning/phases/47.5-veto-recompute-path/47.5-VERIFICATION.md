# Phase 47.5: Veto Recompute Path — Retrospective Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** A company whose enrichment inputs are complete can have its veto recomputed
on demand — without degrading, blanking, or falsifying its data first, and without provider,
research or Anthropic cost — closing the defect where `Company Gate`'s `skip` verdict
silently prevented `Decide Company Action` (the sole writer of `lv_anti_icp_flag` /
`lv_anti_icp_reason`) from ever running on a complete record.

**Verified:** 2026-08-19 (retrospective — phase ran and was sealed 2026-08-12 with no
verifier run at the time; this is that missing gate, ~1 week after the fact)

**Status:** passed

**Note on retrospective method:** this is not a fresh build being verified against a live
system in the same session. It is six already-sealed plans, each carrying its own execution-
level live proof recorded at the time. The method here is (1) reconstruct the four must-haves
from REQUIREMENTS.md + PLAN frontmatter, (2) check the current codebase for the artifacts and
wiring the plans claim to have built, (3) treat the phase's own committed evidence files as
primary sources rather than the SUMMARY narrations of them, and (4) where the underlying
system is still reachable read-only, re-fetch the headline evidence live rather than trust the
committed transcript. All four headline n8n executions this phase cites were re-fetched live
during this verification (see Evidence Tiers below) — this went further than the "internally
consistent, not re-fetchable" caveat the task anticipated.
