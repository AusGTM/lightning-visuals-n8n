## Evidence discipline

The plan's threat T-47.5-16 is "a flip decided on an unresearched assertion", and a hallucinated
quote wearing a citation is the same threat. So every quote a verdict rests on was **independently
re-fetched and grepped**, not taken from the search tool's report:

- ABN Lookup (both companies) and NZ Companies Office (both searches + the Ironman NZ company page)
  were fetched directly and matched verbatim. These carry both verdicts.
- `au.ironmanstore.com` is JS-rendered and `gravitymedia.com` is behind a Cloudflare challenge —
  neither could be re-fetched. Their claims are recorded as **corroboration only, labelled
  self-described**, and no verdict depends on them. The Ironman privacy-policy ABN and postcode do
  match the verified registry record.

## The AU-vs-ANZ tension (surfaced, not silently resolved)

D-V6's table defines `AU` as *domiciled in Australia* and `ANZ` as *multinational with a substantive
ANZ operating presence*; this plan's Task 2 wording said `ANZ` only where operations span both
countries. For Gravity Media (UK-domiciled, AU-only operations) those point at different values.
Rather than picking one, the evidence file handed the conflict to the checkpoint — which is what the
`other-value` option existed for. The operator chose D-V6's reading: **ANZ absorbs the
multinational-with-local-operations case.** Scoring impact is nil either way (`AU`, `NZ` and `ANZ`
all map to the same non-veto region key).

Recorded alongside it, at the operator's instruction: **Gravity Media's NZ leg is unproven**, so
`ANZ` on that record is not later misread as two-country evidence.
