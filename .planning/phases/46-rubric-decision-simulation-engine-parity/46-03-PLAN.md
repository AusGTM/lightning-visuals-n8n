<objective>
Write the decision record RUBRIC-01 requires, and get the operator's sign-off before anything is
committed to an engine.

Purpose: RUBRIC-01's bar is that the decision is *made and evidenced*, not that it follows the
evidence. All three levers here override the closed-deal evidence on operator GTM direction, which
makes the record's honesty the whole deliverable — a document that quietly edits the evidence to
agree with the new weight destroys the record the next recalibration depends on (D-14).

D-05 makes the operator the locking event: Claude proposes, the operator signs off. Everything
downstream — the config commit, the live flow PUT, the doc sync — is gated on Task 2's blocking
checkpoint.

Output: `46-DECISION.md` following the `39-DECISION.md` precedent, and a recorded operator
decision.
</objective>
