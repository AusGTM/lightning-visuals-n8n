<objective>
Commit the signed-off weights to the rubric of record and land them identically in the HubSpot
engine — proven by the parity harness, not by inspection.

Purpose: RUBRIC-03's bar. A weight that lands in one engine only is the split-brain failure v0.7
already paid for once. `config/icp_scoring.yaml` is the rubric of record (see Plan 01's
`<assumption_delta_decision>`); every other engine is a derived representation whose only
permitted relationship to it is "proven equal by test".

Output: the three weight changes committed, the two HubSpot flow archives updated and PUT live
with a running-content read-back, and every stale test literal corrected to its new true value.

**This plan opens the parity red window.** The instant `config/icp_scoring.yaml` carries the new
weights, `scripts/run_scoring_parity.py` — which samples real companies — compares their
old-weight live scores against the new-weight oracle, and every re-tiered record becomes a
`real_finding`. That is correct, expected behaviour of a working guard, not a new bug. The window
closes when Phase 49 re-scores the population. `46-DECISION.md` records which sequencing option
was chosen; follow it.

**If the operator overrode any value at Plan 03's checkpoint, this plan must be revised before it
executes.** The concrete values below are the recommended ones; the sign-off block in
`46-DECISION.md` is authoritative.
