<objective>
Build the match lane's client half: rows get ids once, chunks go out unarmed, and every answer
the backend can give — including the two ways it can decline to answer — comes back as its own
distinguishable state.

Purpose: this is the step that stops the operator paying to enrich a contact HubSpot already
holds. It spends nothing and writes nothing, which is why it needs no arming — but "needs no
arming" has to be structural, visible to the guard that enforces arming everywhere else, and
bounded by tests that stop the allowlist entry becoming a rubber stamp.

Output: `scripts/preingest.py` with `build_rows_spec`, `fetch_matches`, `match_batch` and
`classify_matches`; a `match` capability row; the guard allowlist entry with its two keepers.
</objective>
