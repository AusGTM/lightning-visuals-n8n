Give the flow an operator-facing entry point, and pin the two properties that make it safe as
tests over the document rather than as intentions inside it.

Purpose: the two arming phrases are the phase's central safety design, and they are enforced by
the ORDER of a markdown document. That is fine — the enriched preview must land in the
operator's turn before the ingest arm can be spoken — but only if the order is pinned. A later
edit that moves one heading above another would silently collapse the design.

lane arming no other.
