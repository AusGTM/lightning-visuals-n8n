Render the two previews this flow adds: the cost preview over rows that are not yet HubSpot
records, and the enriched preview the operator reads before granting the write.

Purpose: the enriched preview is the reason there are two arming phrases. It is the operator's
one chance to see what enrichment actually found before any of it becomes a HubSpot record, and
it is where a held row gets named rather than counted.

Output: a rows branch in `records_block`, a file-path `__main__`, and
`preingest.render_enriched_preview`.
</objective>
